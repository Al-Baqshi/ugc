# «العنوان مش عنوان… هو قصة» (The address is a story)

**Truth:** in Lebanon an address is a set of landmarks, and half of them are gone: "next to the bakery, after the shop that used to be a pharmacy, first turn after the big tree… the one they cut down".
**Twist:** escalation. Every landmark is more outdated than the last, while a confused driver keeps calling back. The payoff: on Lebalist, things are sorted by governorate, district and area, so "near me" really is near you.
**Target:** anyone in Lebanon who's ever given or followed directions. Reels, TikTok, WhatsApp Status. After watching: browse listings in your own area.
**Job:** organic (recognition, shares).

**Idea test:** R5 S5 O3 Sh3 = **16/20**. The joke is strong, but the product link (area filtering) is softer than in the other two. It works best as a second video, or as a payoff that shows a real listing pinned in a real area.

**Hooks (lead pick ⭐):**
1. ⭐ «بلبنان العنوان مش عنوان… هو قصة.» · visual: a map with no street names; a pin wanders.
2. Dialogue cold-open: «حدّ الفرن، بعد الدكانة اللي كانت صيدلية…» «أيّا فرن؟»
3. Call-out: «إذا عمرك وصفت بيتك بـ"الشجرة الكبيرة"… هيدا إلك.»

**Beats (rough, ~25 s):** hook → three escalating landmarks with the driver calling back («وين صرت؟») → «الشجرة الكبيرة… اللي قطعوها» → Lebalist: pick governorate → district → area, the listing is right there → CTA ❓.

**Notes:** keep landmarks neutral (bakery, petrol station, shop, tree). No churches, mosques, party offices or checkpoints.
