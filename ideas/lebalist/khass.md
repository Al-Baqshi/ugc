# «خاص» (The DM graveyard)

**Truth:** sell anything in a Lebanese Facebook group and you get forty comments saying «خاص» or «بكم؟» (when the price is in the post), one «آخر سعر؟», one three-minute voice note, and no buyer.
**Twist:** the comments flood the screen faster and faster until the fridge photo disappears under them. Then a calm, single Lebalist listing: price, area, condition, one chat with the person who actually wants it.
**Target:** people selling second-hand goods (furniture, appliances, phones). Reels, TikTok, Facebook. After watching: post the thing on Lebalist instead.
**Job:** organic (recognition, shares; people tag the friend who always comments «خاص»).

**Idea test:** R5 S5 O3 Sh5 = **18/20**. Ownable is the weak spot: Facebook Marketplace could claim the same. It rests on the area sorting and Lebanon-only categories, so the payoff has to show those.

**Hooks (lead pick ⭐):**
1. ⭐ Funniest line first: «نزّلت البراد عالفيسبوك… ٤٧ واحد كتبولي "خاص". وما حدا اشترى.» · visual: a generic group post (not Facebook's look) with the comment counter ticking up. · text: «٤٧ × خاص».
2. Question: «ليش كل ما تكتب السعر… حدا بيسأل "بكم؟"»
3. Pattern break: frame 1 is just the word «خاص» repeated to fill the screen.

**Beats (rough, ~25 s):**
- 0–3 hook as above.
- 3–12 escalation: «بكم؟ — السعر مكتوب. آخر سعر؟ — هيدا آخر سعر. بتوصّلو عالضيعة؟ وواحد بعتلي voice note تلات دقايق… عن خالتو.»
- 12–21 payoff: «حطّيتو على Lebalist: السعر، المنطقة، الحالة. حكاني واحد بس… وإجا أخدو.» (real posting flow in Arabic)
- 21–25 CTA ❓.

**Tools:** motion kit (the comment flood is the visual), real app screens, a real Lebanese voice.
**Notes:** "Facebook" is said, but nothing on screen copies Facebook's design. Don't promise a fast sale.
