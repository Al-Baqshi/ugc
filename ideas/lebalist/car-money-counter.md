# «ماكينة عدّ مصاري» (The money-counting machine): cars + escrow

**Owner brief (2026-11-14):** a car video that brings in the escrow: list your car, and the payment is collected for you. "Our escrow, BitRegalo, for the marketplace, is a perfect solution." Same story-first rule as LB01: the message is hidden.
**Truth:** selling a used car in Lebanon is a cash operation full of suspicion: buyers who never show up, "send me a photo from underneath", a meeting at a petrol station, a bag of dollars counted three times, each note held up to the light.
**Twist:** the uncle turns the sale into a security operation, with a counting machine, a fake-note pen and a UV light. His son sold his own car the same Friday while drinking coffee. «ما في شي ينعدّ. المصاري بتوصل… لمّا السيارة توصل.» (Nothing to count. The money arrives… when the car arrives.) That line is the escrow, said by a character, never pitched.
**Target:** people in Lebanon buying or selling a used car; secondary, anyone who has ever counted dollars on a car bonnet. Reels, TikTok, Facebook.
**Idea test:** R5 S5 O4 Sh4 = **18/20**.

**English gloss:** To sell a car in Lebanon… you need a money-counting machine. My uncle put his car up for sale. First guy: "Send me a photo of the engine… and underneath… and inside the dashboard." Second guy: "I'll come tomorrow at ten." He didn't. Not tomorrow, not the day after. Finally: a meeting at the petrol station. A bag of dollars. My uncle counts note by note… against the light, through the machine, with the fake-note pen. The buyer inspects the car… my uncle inspects the buyer. And my cousin? Sold his car the same Friday… while drinking coffee. My uncle: "Where's the bag? Did you count the money?" He said: "Nothing to count. The money arrives… when the car arrives." Download Lebalist.

## Before this can be published (blocking)
- **Escrow is not live.** Lebalist launched with payments switched off, and BitRegalo runs only on a public **test network** and is **not independently audited**. Its own rule is that nothing of real value should pass through it yet. A video that shows a real car's money being held is a claim we can't make today. We can produce it now and publish only when car payments through escrow actually work in the app.
- **Naming BitRegalo:** the voice-over doesn't name it, keeping the hidden-message rule. If the owner wants it named, use the end card («الدفعة محفوظة مع BitRegalo») only once it is on a main network. Until then, BitRegalo's honesty lines (test network, not audited) would have to appear, which is why the voice-over keeps the escrow general. ❓ Owner's call.
- **Lebanon rules:** check with a Lebanese lawyer whether holding buyers' money for car sales needs a licence there. Payments would also need a local method (OMT, Whish, bank) to fund the escrow.
- Don't show a real number plate, a real face as a "seller", or a currency joke beyond dollars in cash. The banking crisis stays off-limits.
