# «بعرفلك واحد» (I know a guy)

**Truth:** in Lebanon you don't find a plumber, you find "someone who knows someone". You ask your mum, she asks the neighbour, the neighbour forwards a contact card from 2019, and the number belongs to someone who moved abroad.
**Twist:** escalation. The forwarded number travels through more and more relatives, every step more confident, and lands on a man who left the country years ago. The payoff is the opposite: open Lebalist, pick your area, and the plumbers near you are right there.
**Target:** adults in Lebanon who've just had a leak, a power cut or a broken tile. Instagram Reels, TikTok, WhatsApp Status. After watching: send it to the relative who always "knows a guy", then look up a tradesperson by area.
**Job:** organic (recognition, shares).

**Idea test:** R5 S5 O4 Sh5 = **19/20**. Ownable because trades sorted by your area (construction category: plumber, electrician, tiler, painter…) is a real Lebalist feature, not a sticker.
