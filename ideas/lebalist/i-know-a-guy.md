# «بعرفلك واحد» (I know a guy)

**Truth:** in Lebanon you don't find a plumber, you find "someone who knows someone". You ask your mum, she asks the neighbour, the neighbour forwards a contact card from 2019, and the number belongs to someone who moved abroad.
**Twist:** escalation. The forwarded number travels through more and more relatives, every step more confident, and lands on a man who left the country years ago. The payoff is the opposite: open Lebalist, pick your area, and the plumbers near you are right there.
**Target:** adults in Lebanon who've just had a leak, a power cut or a broken tile. Instagram Reels, TikTok, WhatsApp Status. After watching: send it to the relative who always "knows a guy", then look up a tradesperson by area.
**Job:** organic (recognition, shares).

**Idea test:** R5 S5 O4 Sh5 = **19/20**. Ownable because trades sorted by your area (construction category: plumber, electrician, tiler, painter…) is a real Lebalist feature, not a sticker.

**Hooks (lead pick ⭐):**
1. ⭐ Contrarian/funniest line first: «بلبنان ما في سنكري… في "بعرفلك واحد".» · visual: a WhatsApp-style chat (generic, not WhatsApp's look) with the forwarded contact card «أبو سمير سنكري ٢٠١٩». · text: «بعرفلك واحد 👍».
2. Mid-action: «ماما عطيتني رقم سنكري… طلع بألمانيا.» · visual: the call screen, then «Guten Tag?».
3. Call-out: «إذا عندك قريب "بيعرف واحد" لكل شي… هيدا إلك.»

**Beats (rough, ~30 s):**
- 0–3 hook: «بلبنان ما في سنكري… في "بعرفلك واحد".»
- 3–14 escalation: «سألت إمي. إمي سألت جارتنا. جارتنا سألت صهرها. وصلني رقم "أبو سمير سنكري ٢٠١٩". دقّيت… أبو سمير صار بألمانيا من خمس سنين.»
- 14–24 payoff: «فتحت Lebalist. نقّيت منطقتي. سنكري، كهربجي، معلّم بلاط… كلّن حدّي، وبحكيهن مباشرة.» (real app screens in Arabic: construction category → area filter → listing)
- 24–30 CTA: ❓ «فوت على lebalist.com» · callback: Mum texts «لقيت؟ بعرفلك واحد تاني» (found one? I know another guy).

**Tools:** real app screens from the simulator in Arabic; the motion kit for the chat and the forwarded contact cards; a real Lebanese voice (owner/friend) or a tested AI voice; one unbroken take.
**Notes:** names stay sect-neutral («أبو سمير»). The person abroad is just "abroad", no joke about emigration pain. Check that construction listings exist in the live data, and don't show the 7 demo seed listings as real. If calls aren't fixed, say «بحكيهن» (I talk to them) over chat, not a call.

**English gloss:** In Lebanon there's no plumber, there's "I know a guy." Water's leaking under the sink. I asked Mum, Mum asked the neighbour, the neighbour asked her son-in-law, and he sent me a number: "Abu Samir, plumber, 2019." I called… he answered in German. Abu Samir moved to Germany five years ago. I opened Lebalist and picked my area: plumber, electrician, tiler, all near me, and I talk to them directly. Then Mum texts: "Found one? I know another guy"… No, Mum, enough. Go to lebalist.com.

**Notes on the script:**

- «كلّن قريبين منّي» needs real plumber, electrician and tiler listings in one area for the screen recording, or the screens marked "Illustrative". Don't show the demo seed listings as real.
- The names are sect-neutral; Germany is just "abroad", with no joke about emigration.
