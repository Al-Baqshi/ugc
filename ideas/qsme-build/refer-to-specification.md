# Refer to specification.

**Situation:** An RFI asks what fire rating the stair-core walls need. The response comes back: 'Refer to specification.' The specification is a 286-page PDF and pricing closes tomorrow.

**Truth:** 'Refer to specification' is technically always correct and practically never an answer.

**Twist:** The QS asks the drawing set directly and gets an answer with the page attached. Then the product's own Beta line tells them to check it, which is the most QS thing software has ever said.

## Beats
1. 0–3s HOOK.
2. 3–7s The page counter flies from 1/286 to 286/286. Caption: 'It's in there. Somewhere.' VO: 'It's in there somewhere. Page two hundred and something.'
3. 7–12s PRODUCT (real QSME): 'Read a document' on a demo drawing set. 'Finding the sections…' gives way to section chips appearing one by one and '4 of 11 sections ready' (time-cut shown). Caption: 'QSME reads the set into sections.'
4. 12–18s PRODUCT: ask 'What fire rating is specified for the stair core walls?' and show the recorded answer carrying its real 'From your documents (…, p.N)' label. Caption: 'It answers with the page.'
5. 18–21s PRODUCT: a Documents search for 'fire rating' shows the snippet under the matching file. Caption: 'Or search inside every file.'
6. 21–25s The chat's Beta line on screen: 'We're still working on this one. It can get things wrong, so check anything you're going to price or sign off.' Caption: 'It gives you the page so you can check it. Check it.' VO: 'It even tells you to check it. Finally, software that thinks like a QS.'
7. 25–28s Callback card: 'Refer to specification, p.N.' Book a 30-minute demo, qsme.build.

## Hook layers
- **Text:** RFI response: 'Refer to specification.' (286 pages.)
- **Verbal:** Refer to specification. Lovely. Which page?
- **Visual:** A generic RFI form whose response field holds three words, then a PDF page counter whirring from 1 to 286.
- **Audio:** A long scroll-wheel whirr

## Features (verified status at ideation)

- Ask the plans: AI chat that labels where every number came from (Beta) (live)
- Search inside files (live)

**CTA:** Book a 30-minute demo, qsme.build

**Why now:** Plan reading and the source-labelled chat are live on Global, and 'plans that answer with the page cited' is in the brief. The ownable moment is the source label plus the check-it line, not generic AI chat.

## Risks
- The chat is Beta and its source labels are a prompt instruction, not enforced. Show the Beta line; never 'always cites' or 'nothing is guessed'; no fabricated citations, only a real recorded answer.
- Read my plans is PDF-only (up to 300 pages / 50 MB), needs a paid plan, uses AI credits and 'can take a few minutes'. Show a visible time-cut and never say 'instant'.
- Say 'drawing set', never 'consent pack'. The chat defaults to NZ terms and NZD on Global, so ask a non-money question.
- It reads what the drawings say; it doesn't measure or do takeoff.
- We never show who wrote 'Refer to specification', so no consultant is mocked.
