# Portal login #47

**Situation:** Monday 7:10am in a ute. A ceilings subcontractor gets three tender invitations from three main contractors. Each asks him to create an account, set a password with a symbol and fill in a company profile before he can see a drawing. The back page of his diary already lists 46 portal logins, all the same password with a different number on the end.

**Truth:** Your best subcontractors don't want your software. They want to price the job and get back to site, and they quietly decline tenders that cost them a login.

**Twist:** The diary page of 46 logins gets a 47th. Then the video counts three ways QSME meets him where he already is: his inbox, a link, the gate. It punches at portals, never at the subcontractor.

## Beats
1. 0–3s HOOK.
2. 3–8s Three generic invitation screens stack up, each saying 'Create an account to view this tender.' Caption: 'Three invitations. Three new accounts. Zero drawings seen.'
3. 8–13s #1 PRODUCT (real QSME): the main contractor drops his emailed PDF onto 'Add a bid you received', checks it, and it appears as 'Received by you' in the bids table. Caption: '1. Emailed it? It still goes in the comparison.'
4. 13–19s #2 PRODUCT: his phone opens the subcontract link; he ticks, types and draws, and sees 'Signed. Download a copy below and keep it.' Caption: '2. Signs from a link on his phone.'
5. 19–25s #3 PRODUCT: a laminated QR poster cable-tied to a site fence. A gloved hand scans it, then 'Sign in for today' and 'You're signed in'. Caption: '3. One scan at the gate.'
6. 25–30s End card: 'Meet your subbies where they already are.' Book a 30-minute demo at qsme.build.

**Why now:** It folds the QR-gate beat from the inbox's 'Who was on site Tuesday?' (14) into a stronger truth. All three moments shipped 19–23 September, and it answers a main contractor's first objection to any platform: 'my subbies won't use it'.

## Risks
- Don't claim subcontractors never need an account (bidding inside QSME is by invitation), and don't put 'no account', 'no login' or 'no app' on screen or in the VO (owner rule, 2026-10-03). The claim is that an emailed quote still goes in, signing happens from a link and the gate is one scan.
- No pricing lines at all (no 'per-seat', 'never pay to bid' or fee wording; owner rule, 2026-10-03). Team seats aren't available yet, so don't imply several staff on one organisation account.
- The site sign-in page footer is NZ-worded, so crop it. No compliance, ID-verification or sign-out-time claims, and invented names on any register.
- Received-bid screens may say 'GST' on Global, so crop the headers or use a demo org from a GST country.
- No real or recognisable portal brands and no competitor names. The diary passwords are blurred props.
