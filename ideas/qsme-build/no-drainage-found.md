# Heads up: no drainage found

**Situation:** Week three on site, the groundworks subcontractor asks which sheet shows the stormwater drainage. No sheet does. The drainage layout was never in the 140-page drawing set that went to tender, and nobody had read the set end to end to notice.

**Truth:** Nobody reads the whole drawing set, so the gap gets found on site after the tender, which is where it costs the most.

**Twist:** After reading every page, the most useful thing QSME says is what it didn't find: 'Heads up: no drainage found in this pack.' It says this before the tender goes out. Then it points each bidder at the right pages instead of sending everyone the whole set.

## Beats
1. 0–3s HOOK: kinetic page-flick of a demo drawing set ends on the back cover. Caption: 'Found on site, week 3: no drainage layout.'
2. 3–8s PROBLEM: 2s AI b-roll cutaway of an open trench. Caption: 'We found out on site. After the tender.' VO (Kiwi, later): 'We found out on site. After the tender. Classic.'

4. 17–24s THE MOMENT: notification 'Your plans have been read — … sections ready to explore. Heads up: no drainage found in this pack.' Caption: 'The useful bit is what it didn't find.'

6. 30–36s TENDER: 'Point them at the right sheets': tick the sections, and the bidder sees 'Referenced drawing pages'. Then 'Save these pages as a file' makes a 3-page PDF for one trade. Caption: 'Each bidder gets their pages, not all 140.'
7. 36–42s HONEST LINE + CTA: 'It reads what the drawings say. It doesn't measure them.' End card: 'Book a 30-minute demo · qsme.build'.

## Risks
- Needs a paid plan or the trial plus AI credits (1 per 10 pages): never call it 'free'. It is not instant: keep the 'sped up' tag.
- It reads; it doesn't measure or take off. Mark-up and 'Open drawing' buttons are switched off, so keep them out of frame.
- Say 'drawing set' in captions and VO. The on-screen notification saying 'pack' is fine; never say 'consent pack'.
- Detection isn't guaranteed, so the demo must be a real run with no mock-up. Never say 'nothing is guessed' or '100% accurate'.
- Protected PDFs may refuse page-splitting: test the demo set first. Leave the AI chat out because it defaults to NZ terms.
