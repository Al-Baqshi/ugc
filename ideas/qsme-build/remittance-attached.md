# Remittance attached. (It wasn't.)

**Situation:** Month-end. The subcontractor's accounts team writes 'Claim 6 not received.' The main contractor's accounts team replies 'Paid on the 14th. Remittance attached.' There is no attachment. Both spreadsheets are right, according to themselves.

**Truth:** Half of payment admin is two people proving to each other whether money moved.

**Twist:** The thread escalates through every familiar reply ('Nothing attached?', 'Reattached', 'That's March's', 'Which account?') until someone says 'Can we jump on a call?'

## Beats
1. 0–3s HOOK.
2. 3–9s The thread escalates one reply per beat: 'Nothing attached?' · 'Reattached' · 'That's March's' · 'Which account?' · 'Can we jump on a call?' VO: 'Reattached. That's March. Which account?'
3. 9–12s Split screen: two generic spreadsheets, claim 6 reading 'PAID' on one and 'OUTSTANDING' on the other. Caption: 'Both correct. According to themselves.'
4. 12–16s PRODUCT (real QSME): the contractor's milestone panel, 'Submit progress' with a site photo and their invoice.
5. 16–21s The payer clicks 'I paid outside QSME', enters a 'Payment reference', ticks 'I have sent the money.' and presses 'Mark as paid', which shows 'Awaiting confirmation'. Caption: 'Only the payer can mark it paid.'
6. 21–24s The receiver ticks 'The money has cleared in my account.', giving 'Settled outside QSME and confirmed by both sides.' A flash of the Project money page shows 'Cost paid' updating. Caption: 'Only the receiver can confirm it.'
7. 24–27s Caption: 'QSME never touches the money. It keeps the one record you both ticked.' Book a 30-minute demo, qsme.build.

## Hook layers
- **Text:** 'Remittance attached.' (It wasn't.)

## Features (verified status at ideation)

- Project money page: estimate vs actual, every stage, your own money in and out (live)

**Why now:** On Global, 'recorded, never moved' is the only way to settle, so the honest version is the whole story. Month-end claims come round every month, and no owner fact is pending.

## Risks

- Global has no bank button, and no Dispute button may appear on screen.
- Never reuse payments.mp4 or awarded-work.mp4 audio or visuals ('release the funds', 'platform fee').
- Hide currency and GST labels on the milestone panel.
- The accounts teams on both sides are not the butt; the spreadsheets are.
