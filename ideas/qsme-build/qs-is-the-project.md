# QS is the project (what QSME stands for)

**Owner brief (2026-10-04):** say what QSME stands for — QS, quantity surveying — and that the QS is the most important part of every project: a good QS means a good project. QSME is the platform for the QS (professional QS or anyone doing the QS job) to run the project from A to Z: planning, drawings, estimating and quantities, tendering, contracts, delivery, documents, project management. Edition: qsme.build.

**Truth:** every project has someone holding the numbers, the scope and the paper together; when that goes wrong, everything goes wrong.

**Guardrails:** QS = quantity surveying; don't invent what "ME" stands for (ask the owner). No takeoff/measuring-on-drawings claims (mark-up is switched off) — say quantities drafted from your drawings, checked by you. Bids compared as totals excl./incl. tax, not a line-by-line matrix. Never "AI replaces the QS". Global CTA: Book a 30-minute demo at qsme.build. No "free".
