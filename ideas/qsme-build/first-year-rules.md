# Same rules as my first year

**Situation:** 10:40pm. An estimator wants the AI assistant they already use to draft tomorrow's plumbing & drainage tender from the project. The commercial manager has one question: 'What can it actually do without asking?'

**Truth:** Nobody in construction is scared of AI typing. Everyone is scared of AI sending.

**Twist:** The connector's 'What it can never do' list reads exactly like the rules for a first-year cadet QS, and the founder admits that's mostly what they did too: the typing.

## Beats
1. 0–3s HOOK.
2. 3–10s The list, one stamp per line, in the exact /connect wording: 'Move money or record a payment' ✕ · 'Send or publish a tender, or accept a bid' ✕ · 'Issue, sign or cancel a contract' ✕ · 'Change the schedule or delete a project' ✕.
3. 10–13s Caption: 'Same rules I had in my first year as a QS.' VO: 'Funny. Same rules I had in my first year.'
4. 13–19s PRODUCT: the owner's own assistant, connected to a demo Global org, gets the prompt 'Draft a tender for the plumbing and drainage jobs, private, closing next Friday.' and replies that a draft is ready to review.
5. 19–23s PRODUCT: in QSME the tender sits as a draft while a human cursor hovers over the publish step. Caption: 'It drafts. You decide.'
6. 23–27s Settings → Connected apps → 'Recent activity — What your connected apps have done, newest first.' Caption: 'And it keeps a list of what it did.'
7. 27–30s The /connect line: 'It never moves money, sends a tender or accepts anything. You stay in charge; it just does the typing.' Kicker: 'Also my first year.' Book a 30-minute demo, qsme.build.

## Hook layers
- **Text:** I connected AI to our projects. Here's what it's not allowed to do.
- **Verbal:** I connected AI to our projects. Here's the list of things it's not allowed to do.
- **Visual:** Red crosses stamp onto a list, one per line, taken from the public /connect page's 'What it can never do' column.
- **Audio:** A rubber-stamp thunk per line

## Features (verified status at ideation)
- Bring your own AI assistant: Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor (MCP) (live)
- Tender cut from an SOQ pocket: private or public, invitations and share link (live)

## Footage plan

- kinetic: Stamp animation over the list, captions, end card
- owner-recording: Week 2: the owner's own assistant connected to a demo Global org drafting the plumbing & drainage tender; the draft waiting unpublished in QSME; Settings → Connected apps → Recent activity (no tokens, no setup command, no real projects)
- filmed: Optional: the founder to camera for the 3-second 'same rules as my first year' line, only if the founder films it; otherwise captions only

**CTA:** Book a 30-minute demo, qsme.build

**Why now:** This improves the inbox idea 'I asked Claude to write my tender' (15) with a sharper truth (the fear is sending, not typing). The connector has been live since 30 August and its 'can never do' list is public.

## Risks
- No partnership with or endorsement by Anthropic or OpenAI. Show the assistant as the user's own tool, with no vendor logo as the hero.
- ChatGPT needs a paid plan with developer mode; free ChatGPT doesn't work.
- Never 'summarise the bids' (qsme.build's AI page says it, so don't capture that page). No tool counts, tokens or setup command on screen.
- The connector works in NZD terms via api.qsme.co.nz, so crop URLs and avoid money questions.
- Confirm the founder is happy to voice 'my first year as a QS'. The joke is on the founder's own first year, never on graduates.
