# Column H (hidden, not deleted)

**Situation:** Friday 4:40pm: an estimator emails the plasterboard & ceilings BoQ to six subcontractors for pricing. The client's pre-tender estimate is still in column H, hidden but not deleted. Two weeks later all six bids land between 1% and 2% under it.

**Truth:** Every QS has either sent the priced BoQ by mistake or lain awake wondering if they did. In a spreadsheet, 'hidden' is one right-click away from 'unhidden'.

**Twist:** The market is suddenly, eerily accurate. The joke isn't on the bidders. It's on us, finding out column H was never really hidden.

## Beats
1. 0–3s HOOK: totals stack beside 'Our estimate 486,200' (fictional). Caption: 'Six bids. All just under our estimate.' VO (later, dry Kiwi QS): 'Six bids. Every one just under our estimate.'
2. 3–7s Caption: 'What a well-informed market.' The sent spreadsheet's column letters read F | G | I, with no H. VO: 'Remarkably well-informed market, this.'
3. 7–11s Right-click on the G|I border, then 'Unhide'. Column H slides open: 'CLIENT ESTIMATE (DO NOT SEND)', every line priced. Caption: 'Column H. Hidden. Not deleted.'
4. 11–14s The sent email: 'Please find attached BoQ for pricing.' To: 6 recipients · Fri 4:40pm. VO: 'Hidden isn't the same as gone.'
5. 14–21s PRODUCT (real QSME, demo org) split screen. Left: our SOQ pocket with rates and totals. Right: the invited bidder's view of the same lines, quantities shown, rate column empty. Caption: 'In QSME, bidders get your lines and quantities. Never your rates. Never your budget.'
6. 21–25s PRODUCT: the live qsme.build/security sentence highlighted: 'Rates, amounts and the tender budget are stripped out server-side, not hidden in the interface.' Caption: 'Stripped. Not hidden.' VO: 'Not hidden. Stripped out before it ever leaves.'
7. 25–28s End card: 'Private tenders, by invitation.' Book a 30-minute demo, qsme.build. VO: 'Q-S-me dot build.'

## Risks

- Bidders may still quote a single total, so never say every bidder itemises.
- Don't imply QSME stops every leak (people can still forward files). The claim is limited to rates and budget never being sent to bidders.
- Generic spreadsheet and mail UI only: no Microsoft logos or ribbon. Skit figures are fiction and the demo data is invented.
- Quote the security sentence exactly, with no paraphrase upward.
