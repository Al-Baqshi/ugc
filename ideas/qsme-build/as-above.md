# Wednesday: As above.

**Situation:** Friday 4:52pm in the site office. The site manager writes the whole week's diary from memory. Monday gets a paragraph. Tuesday: 'Works progressed.' Wednesday: 'As above.' Thursday: 'As above.' Friday: 'Ditto.' It rained on Tuesday until 11.

**Truth:** The site diary is written for the claim you hope never happens, at the moment you're least able to remember the week.

**Twist:** The entries shrink day by day down to 'Ditto'. Nine months later someone asks whether Tuesday 13 October was a rain day, and the diary says 'Works progressed.'

## Beats
1. 0–3s HOOK.
2. 3–8s The week shrinks: MON (a full paragraph) → TUE 'Works progressed.' → WED 'As above.' → THU 'As above.' → FRI 'Ditto.' VO: 'Monday gets a paragraph. Wednesday gets as above.'
3. 8–11s Card: 'Nine months later.' A generic email: 'Can you confirm whether Tue 13 Oct was a rain day?'
4. 11–13s The diary's Tuesday: 'Works progressed.' Caption: 'It was. Probably.' VO: 'It was. I think.'
5. 13–19s PRODUCT (real QSME): log a 'Rain day' for Tue 13 Oct on the programme and press 'Push these tasks'. The 'What happened' panel shows 'Rain day — Tue 13 Oct'. Cut to the Daily log: 'Rain day' and 'Schedule change' entries already sit under Tuesday. Caption: 'Log the rain day once. It writes itself into the diary.'
6. 19–23s A chat photo is long-pressed, 'Mark as proof of work', and it appears in that day's log. Then 'Add a note for today…' and 'Add photo' on the phone. Caption: 'Photos land on the day you post them. Not on Friday.'
7. 23–26s Caption: 'It doesn't write the whole diary. It writes the bits you'd forget by Friday.' Book a 30-minute demo, qsme.build.

**Why now:** Rain days and schedule moves started writing themselves into the daily log in September. It's the brief's 'site diaries written on Friday for Monday', and Northern Hemisphere winter (rain-day season) is starting. Tue 13 Oct 2026 really is a Tuesday.

## Risks
- Only some entries write themselves (rain days, schedule moves, answers, checklist steps, documents, tagged chat). Never say 'it writes itself' without qualification.
- A person logs the rain day and the forecast only shades the chart. Never imply automatic weather capture (some rival diaries do pull weather, which lowers ownability).
- It is not a legal record or an extension-of-time tool. Photos are not GPS- or time-verified, and there is no video upload or offline mode.
- Log the rain day on the web; phone rain-day logging is merged but not confirmed in the store app.
- Keep it a first-person confession so the joke is on us, not on site managers.
