# Day 23 of one signature

**Situation:** The ceilings subcontractor has been on site since Day 2. The subcontract went out 23 days ago. Since then: 'will sign tonight', a resend (it went to junk), a printer out of toner, and finally a sideways scan of pages 1–6 and 8–12. Page 7 is the retention clause.

**Truth:** The subcontract is the one document on the job that everybody agrees with and nobody has signed.

**Twist:** A deadpan day counter where every excuse is innocent and true, ending with a scan that skips exactly the page that matters.

## Beats
1. 0–3s HOOK counter.
2. 3–10s Message stack, each stamped with its day: Day 1 'Sent.' · Day 4 'Will sign tonight' · Day 9 'Can you resend? Went to junk' · Day 15 'Printer's out of toner' · Day 22 'Signed and scanned!' VO: 'Will sign tonight. Went to junk. Out of toner.'
3. 10–13s Day 23: the scan arrives sideways, page strip '1 2 3 4 5 6 8 9…'. Caption: 'Page 7: retention.' VO: 'All there except page seven. Retention. Naturally.'
4. 13–15s Caption: 'They've been on site since Day 2.'
5. 15–21s PRODUCT (real QSME): our own subcontract PDF goes in via 'Sign it as it is', then 'Send for signing'. On a phone: tick 'I have read this agreement and I agree to sign it electronically.', type the name, draw, and 'Signed. Download a copy below and keep it.' Caption: 'One link. Signed on the phone. Nothing to install.'
6. 21–23s The sender's list flips from 'Out for signing' to 'Everyone has signed', with a flash of the certificate page. Caption: 'Every page. One signature.'
7. 23–25s Caption: 'We still can't make them open it. We did make it one link.' Book a 30-minute demo, qsme.build.

## Risks
- Built-in templates are written for NZ law. For Global use 'your own agreement' / 'Sign it as it is'. Never 'legally binding everywhere', 'court-proof' or legal advice.
- No signing order, placed signature boxes, witnesses or initials exist.
- Signing runs in the phone's browser, not the iOS app.
- The certificate page shows network address and device, so use an invented signer and crop anything identifying.
- The excuses stay innocent (junk folder, toner). The subcontractor is not the butt.
- E-signing is common, so ownability rests on 'one link, signed in the phone's browser' plus bringing your own subcontract.
