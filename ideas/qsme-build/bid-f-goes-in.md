# Bid F goes in (Six bids, part 2)

**Situation:** This is the same plasterboard & ceilings tender as 'Six bids. Six formats.'. Bid F, the most complete one, is a phone photo of a whiteboard. At 8pm the QS has to get it into the comparison without retyping 1,240 m² and 380 m² by hand.

**Truth:** The best price often arrives in the worst format, from a subcontractor who will never log into anything. So someone retypes it, and mistypes it.

**Twist:** QSME reads the photo into a draft beside the original, then refuses to save it until a person ticks that they checked it. Its best line admits it can be wrong: 'This was read automatically and can be wrong. Nothing is saved until you have checked it.'

## Beats
1. 0–3s HOOK as described, with a small chip: 'Six bids, part 2'.
2. 3–8s PROBLEM: a generic spreadsheet, a tired cursor retyping the whiteboard lines, and a clock reading 8:04pm. Caption: 'Option A: retype it. Tonight. Correctly. Probably.' VO (Kiwi, later): same line.
3. 8–16s SOLUTION: 'Add a bid you received' ('PDF, saved email (.eml), photo or text file — up to 20 MB') → 'Reading the quote…' → the 'The bid' form beside 'The original' photo. Caption: 'Option B: drop the photo in. QSME reads it into a draft, next to the original.'
4. 16–23s THE HONEST BIT: zoom on 'This was read automatically and can be wrong. Nothing is saved until you have checked it.' If the real read raises a warning such as 'That is not the total price above…', hold on it. Caption: 'It shows you what it read. Then it waits for you.'
5. 23–29s CHECK: the QS checks each figure against the photo and ticks 'I have checked this against the original and it is right.' The bid joins the table with a mail icon and 'Received by you'. Caption: 'Checked by a person. Now it's a bid like the others.'
6. 29–34s VALUE: Bidder F's row sits in the table like every other bid and can be awarded (crop out the 'they don't need a QSME account' helper line). Caption: 'Bidder F is in the comparison, and can still be awarded.' VO: 'And Bidder F's whiteboard is in the running.'
7. 34–40s CTA: the whiteboard now reads '6 formats. 1 table.' in marker. End card: 'Book a 30-minute demo · qsme.build'.

## Hook layers
- **Text:** Bid F is a photo of a whiteboard. It's going in the comparison anyway.
- **Verbal:** Remember Bid F? The whiteboard? It's going in.
- **Visual:** The real whiteboard photo on a phone in the QS's hand: the part-1 prop, with 'Ceilings … INCL' underlined twice. The same file is dragged onto a QSME tender and the panel reads 'Filing the quote…'.
- **Audio:** A marker cap click (callback to part 1), then a soft drop sound.

## Risks
- Test the photo read first. If the AI misreads, show that honestly, because the check step is the point. If it can't read the photo at all, switch to a phone-scanned PDF and rewrite the hook.
- Never show a warning or figure that didn't happen in the real run. No faked UI.
- Compare bids still says 'GST' for global orgs, and the 'calc.' note says 15%: crop both and never say GST. Don't show the line 'They match if the line prices include GST.'
- Don't imply QSME reads your inbox or imports automatically. Outlook .msg files are refused.
