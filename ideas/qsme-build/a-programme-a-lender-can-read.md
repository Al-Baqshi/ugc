# A programme a lender can read

**Situation:** The developer's funder wants an updated programme before the next drawdown review. The PM sends a Gantt screenshot that runs off the page, has no dates on the bars, and repeats one 450,000 contract on six rows, so the cost line reads 2.7 million.

**Truth:** The one question a lender asks, 'what is actually holding this build up?', usually has no answer on the page.

**Twist:** QSME prints a programme a non-builder can read. Every bar has its dates in words, the chain that sets the finish is ringed in purple with a sentence that says so, each contract's money counts once, and estimates are marked '~'. It goes out on your letterhead, and it never pretends a bank approved it.

3. 26–46s 2 · SLIPS AND RAIN: drag a task and the toast reads '3 moved · 2 sent for OK — 5 that follow them came too · Undo'. A booked subcontractor's task waits: 'This needs [name]'s OK — nothing changes for them until they say yes.' Log a rain day and see 'What moves'. VO: 'One slip moves what's linked. Anyone booked is asked, not told.'
4. 46–64s 3 · WHAT DRIVES THE FINISH: switch on the purple chain. Drag a grey task and nothing moves; drag a purple one and the finish moves. VO: 'Purple decides the finish date. Grey has room.'
5. 64–78s 4 · YOUR DAYS OFF: 'Add a day off' for the site's own holidays and shutdown → '12 jobs are planned on days the site is shut' → 'Move them off the days off'. Work moves later, never earlier. VO: 'Public holidays where you are? Add them once as days off.'
6. 78–96s 5 · MONEY, ONCE: tick Cost and Paid. Tender-stage tasks show a grey '~' estimate and awarded ones a solid price, with 'Contracted · Paid · Still to contract ~'. VO: 'Estimates carry a squiggle. Agreed prices don't. And a contract counts once, not six times.'
7. 96–114s 6 · PRINT: 'As a PDF — the chart and the task list' produces an A4 landscape 'Construction programme' on the developer's letterhead, with the boxes 'ON SITE FROM · FINISHES · HOW LONG · TASKS · NEXT MILESTONE' and the sentence 'Any one of them running late moves the whole finish.' Hands slide the printed page across a desk and a finger traces the purple rings.
8. 114–122s CTA: 'Bring your programme spreadsheet to a 30-minute demo · qsme.build'.

## Risks
- Never say 'bank-approved', 'bank-ready' or 'accepted by lenders', and never name a lender. The reader is fictional and hands-only.
- The only built-in public-holiday calendar isn't the viewer's, so frame holidays strictly as 'add your own days off'. Switch off the default shutdown in the demo org.
- Never use 'critical path' as a UI word. No percent-complete, cash-flow forecasting or float.
- Asked-not-told works in-app and by email only, with no SMS. Keep native phone schedule shots out, because they aren't confirmed in the store app.
- Money columns are visible to owners and managers only. The letterhead must be set up first; there is no logo by default.
