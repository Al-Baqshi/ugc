# Column D is hidden

**Situation:** 6:10pm on tender day, a main contractor's estimator emails the façade package to five subcontractors. It goes out as the priced estimate spreadsheet with the Rate and Budget columns 'hidden' (right-click → Hide), and any one of them can right-click → Unhide.

**Truth:** Every estimator has sent, or nearly sent, a tender spreadsheet where the rates were only hidden, then lain awake wondering who clicked Unhide.

**Twist:** In QSME there's nothing to unhide, because the bidder's copy never contained your rates: 'Rates, amounts and the tender budget are stripped out server-side, not hidden in the interface.' The callback: right-click the bidder's empty Rate column and there is simply nothing there.

## Beats
1. 0–3s HOOK: right-click → Unhide, and Rate and Budget reappear. Caption: 'Your rates are in column D. Column D is hidden.'
2. 3–8s PROBLEM: a generic mail 'Sent' view showing the same file sent to Bidders A–E. Caption: 'Sent to five bidders. We've all sent that file.' VO (Kiwi, later): same line.
3. 8–13s PRODUCT INTRO: lower-third 'QSME · tendering & estimating software'. The same façade package as an SOQ pocket with rates and a Grand total. Caption: 'Price it once, in QSME.'
4. 13–21s SOLUTION: split screen. Left: the owner's priced lines. Right: the invited bidder's view of the same lines ('Est Qty / Bid Qty / Rate ($) / Total Cost ($)') with the rate column empty. Caption: 'Bidders get the same lines. Not your rates. Not your budget.'
5. 21–27s MECHANISM: the security-page sentence, verbatim: 'Rates, amounts and the tender budget are stripped out server-side, not hidden in the interface.' Caption: 'Not hidden. Never sent.'
6. 27–31s CALLBACK: on the bidder view, the cursor right-clicks the empty Rate header and gets an ordinary browser menu with no 'Unhide'. Caption: 'Nothing to unhide.'
7. 31–35s CTA end card: 'Bring your spreadsheet to the demo · Book 30 minutes · qsme.build'. VO: 'Bring your spreadsheet. We'll show you where column D goes. Q-S-me dot build.'

## Hook layers
- **Text:** Your rates are in column D. Column D is 'hidden'.
- **Verbal:** Your rates are in column D. Column D is… hidden.
- **Visual:** A generic spreadsheet with no Microsoft branding, 'Facade_Tender_FINAL.xlsx'. The cursor right-clicks the seam between C and E and picks 'Unhide'. Column D unfolds with 'Rate' and 'Budget' in bold. Hold one beat.
- **Audio:** One trackpad click, a soft unfold whoosh as the column opens, then dead air.

## Risks
- Don't imply bidders see nothing. They see line descriptions, units and your estimated quantities, and those quantities are neither measured nor guaranteed.
- Bidders may quote one total instead of itemising, so don't say every bid is itemised.
- /platform/tendering also shows a line-by-line comparison mock, 'mark up the drawings' and 'compares bids line by line'. None of these may appear in frame.
- Keep the joke on the situation and on ourselves ('we've all sent that file'), never on bidders peeking.
- The final cut needs the owner recordings (weeks 2–4). The week-1 animatic uses the labelled mock.
