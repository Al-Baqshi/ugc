# 'I paid you last week'

**Situation:** Month end. The scaffolding subcontractor rings to say Stage 2 hasn't landed; the contractor's accounts team says it went out Friday. Two spreadsheets, two answers, one awkward phone call.

**Truth:** 'I paid you last week' / 'No you didn't': every payment argument starts with two records that disagree.

**Twist:** QSME doesn't move a cent, and that's the point. The payer records it ('I have sent the money.'), only the receiver can confirm it ('The money has cleared in my account.'), and one person can't do both. QSME keeps the record you both agree on, not the cash.

## Beats
1. 0–3s HOOK as described.
2. 3–8s Caption: 'Two spreadsheets. Both right, apparently.'
3. 8–15s The subcontractor's side of the milestone ('Proof of work / Submit progress / Upload invoice'): a photo and an invoice, then 'Submit progress'. Caption: 'Stage 2: the work and the invoice, on the milestone.'
4. 15–22s The contractor sees 'How do you plan to settle this?' → 'Outside QSME — Bank transfer, cash, cheque — whatever suits.' → enters a reference → 'I have sent the money.' Caption: 'Pay the way you already do. Record it here.'
5. 22–28s Both screens read 'Awaiting confirmation'. The subcontractor ticks 'The money has cleared in my account.' → 'Settled outside QSME and confirmed by both sides.' Caption: 'Only the payer marks it paid. Only the receiver confirms it.'
6. 28–35s HONEST LINE + CTA: 'QSME never holds your money. It keeps the record you both agree on.' End card: 'Book a 30-minute demo · qsme.build'.

## Hook layers
- **Text:** 'I paid you Friday.' / 'Nothing's landed.'
- **Verbal:** Paid you Friday. / Nothing's landed, mate.
- **Visual:** Split screen: two generic spreadsheets with the same row, 'Stage 2 — Scaffold'. The left cell says PAID, the right says OWING, and both cursors blink.
- **Audio:** Two notification dings out of sync, then flat silence.

## Features (verified status at ideation)
- Milestones: proof of work, invoice, then 'paid outside QSME' confirmed by the receiver (live)
- Award in one click: the winning bid becomes a contract with milestones and retention (live)

- kinetic: Burned-in captions, the honest line card and end card. Kiwi VO script for later, with two voices for the hook.

**CTA:** Pay the way you already do and keep the proof with the milestone. Book a 30-minute demo at qsme.build.

**Why now:** On the global edition, 'recorded, never moved' is the only way to settle. The honest framing is now simply how the product works.

## Risks
- Never say 'release the funds', escrow, or 'secure' or guaranteed payments. Never use the old payments or awarded-work walkthrough clips, whose VO implies QSME holds money.
- QSME doesn't verify that an outside payment happened; it rests on each side's word. Don't imply any bank check.
- No bank or pay buttons for Global, and no Dispute button on screen.
- Keep the joke on the two spreadsheets, never on the subcontractor or the accounts team.
