# I rang the wrong subcontractor

**Situation:** Monday site meeting on an apartment job. Glazing is four days late and the PM has rung the glazier six times. Meanwhile the lift install, two days late with nobody worried about it, is the trade actually pushing out the handover date.

**Truth:** Every programme has one late trade everyone shouts about and another quietly moving the finish date, and a spreadsheet Gantt can't tell you which is which.

**Twist:** QSME rings the chain that decides the finish date in purple, and Glazing isn't purple. The PM was chasing a trade with room to spare, and now owes the glazier a coffee (a self-own).

## Beats
1. 0–3s HOOK as described.
2. 3–9s The demo schedule. Switch on 'What drives the finish date' and a chain of bars gets purple rings joined by arrows; Glazing has none. Caption: 'Purple = drives the finish date.'
3. 9–16s Drag Glazing three days right. The toast says what moved and the last bar doesn't budge. Caption: 'Glazing has room. Grey means room.'
4. 16–23s Drag Lift install (purple) one day and the finish moves a day. Caption: 'Purple slips a day, the finish slips a day.'
5. 23–29s Kinetic quote from the product's own guide: 'Want to finish sooner? Shortening a grey task does nothing. Shorten a purple one.' VO (Kiwi, later): 'So I rang the lift company. And I owe the glazier a coffee.'
6. 29–35s CTA end card: 'Book a 30-minute demo · qsme.build', with a small line: 'Also prints on the programme PDF.'

## Hook layers
- **Text:** Glazing: 4 days late. Finish date: hasn't moved.

- **Audio:** Six short generic outgoing ring tones, each cut off, then silence.
