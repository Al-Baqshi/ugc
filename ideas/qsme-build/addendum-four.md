# Addendum 4, 4:52pm

**Situation:** Wednesday 4:52pm, and the tender closes Thursday 2pm. Addendum No. 4 (drainage, Rev D) goes out by email to a distribution list that still includes someone who left in March. On Thursday one bidder has priced Rev C, one Rev D and one Rev B, and nobody knows where Rev B came from.

**Truth:** On tender day the bids don't really differ on price. They differ on which revision each bidder happened to open.

**Twist:** The addendum register's 'Received?' column escalates from 'Y' to 'Y?' to 'Probably' to 'He said he'd check'.

## Beats
1. 0–3s HOOK as above.
2. 3–9s The addendum register's 'Received?' column fills: A 'Y' · B 'Y?' · C 'Probably' · D 'He said he'd check'. Caption: 'Addendum register, as at 5:15pm.' VO: 'Did they get it? Yes. Probably. He said he'd check.'
3. 9–14s Thursday: three bid totals land, each stamped with what it priced: 'Rev C' · 'Rev D' · 'Rev B (?)'. Caption: 'Three prices. Three different jobs.' VO: 'Three bids. Three revisions. No idea where Rev B came from.'
4. 14–19s PRODUCT (real QSME): editing an open tender shows '2 bid(s) are in on this scope. Change it and everyone still in the running is told what moved.' Caption: 'Change the scope and every bidder still in is told what moved.'
5. 19–24s PRODUCT: a new version of the drawing set is uploaded (v2 in Documents). The bidder's 'Referenced drawing pages, Drainage layout p.22–24' row turns amber: 'pages may have moved'. Caption: 'New revision? The tender tells them.'
6. 24–27s Back on the register, the 'Received?' column has nothing left to guess. Caption: 'Nobody has to type Probably.' VO: 'Nobody has to type probably any more.'
7. 27–30s End card: 'Same lines for every bidder. A warning when the drawings move.' Book a 30-minute demo, qsme.build.

## Risks
- References are plain rows naming the document and page range and do not open a drawing viewer. Never show bidders clicking into or marking up a drawing (mark-up is switched off).
- Page references need the set read by 'Read my plans' (paid plan). Say 'drawing set', never 'consent pack'.
- Don't claim QSME guarantees everyone prices the latest revision. The claim is that bidders are told what moved and see a warning.
- Notices go in-app and by email only (no SMS), and there is no tender Q&A thread.
- The ex-colleague on the list is our own mess, never a named person.
