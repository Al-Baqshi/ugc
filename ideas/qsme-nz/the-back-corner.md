# The back corner

**Situation:** A first-home buyer at a Saturday open home in Auckland: the front lawn is bone dry, the flyer says 'flat, sunny, easy-care', and nobody mentions the low back corner behind the shed where the grass is a bit too green. The auction is in nine days.

**Truth:** Everyone judges a section from the letterbox. The part that bites is usually out the back, and you find out after you've bid.

**Twist:** The hazard check reads the whole parcel shape, not the front gate, so the back corner counts. A sunny, lovely open home, then one amber chip. The payoff is honest: it's not a LIM, it's the right question to ask before you bid.

## Beats
1. 0-2.5s HOOK: section b-roll, marker circle on the back corner. Text: 'POV: lovely open home. Then you check the back corner.'
2. 2.5-6s: the buyer's notes-app checklist (kinetic, fictional): 'Sunny (tick) Dry (tick) Easy-care (tick) Back corner ?'. VO (Mel, later): 'Front lawn's bone dry. Flyer says easy-care. Just gonna check the back corner, eh.'
3. 6-11s: live capture of qsme.co.nz 'Search your address' with a 'Demo address (Auckland)' tag: 'Looking up the property — boundaries, zoning & services…', the map flies in and the boundary draws.
4. 11-16s: chips flip green one by one ('Water supply — Available · ~3 m'), then one goes amber: 'Flood plain — Present — check' (or whichever hazard chip the demo address returns). Zoom plus a soft thud. Caption: 'Checked against the whole section. Not just the letterbox.'
5. 16-20s: the notes app updates: 'Back corner: ask about the flood plain. Order the LIM.' The footer stays on screen: 'Not a substitute for a LIM, PIM or engineering advice.' VO: 'Not a LIM. Just the right question, before you bid.'

## Risks
- Finding a public, commercial or owner-owned Auckland address that returns an amber chip may take several test lookups. The story is a residential open home, so the screen must carry a 'Demo address' tag.
- Never imply that 'Clear' means won't flood, or that the check replaces a LIM. Keep the footer disclaimer visible.
- Services and hazards are Auckland only. Outside Auckland the page says 'Auckland first, more councils to come'. Say it in the caption.
- Don't reference the 2023 Auckland floods. Too raw for a light video.
- Don't say 'instant'. Lookups take seconds.
- The mobile layout of the homepage map is not yet checked for a clean 9:16 capture.
