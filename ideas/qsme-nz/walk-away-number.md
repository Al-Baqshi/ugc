# The walk-away number

**Situation:** Saturday, 9:40am, in a car park outside an Auckland auction. A first-time small developer is twenty minutes from bidding on a section she has already researched, and everyone inside is about to 'feel it' on the day. She opens the Rough feasibility calculator one last time.

**Truth:** At an auction you set your limit with your heart. Your margin finds out later.

**Twist:** The calculator works backwards. The green box 'What you could pay for the land to keep a 20% margin' is a walk-away number, worked out in the car before the room gets loud. Put in the builder's real rate and the margin goes red: the most useful buzzkill in property.

1. 0–3s HOOK (filmed). CAPTION: '20 minutes before the auction. One number.' VO: 'Twenty minutes out. Worked out my walk-away number.'
2. 3–9s: the section's Site research, 'Development potential' with 'Homes with consent — indicative range', scrolling to 'Rough feasibility — Your numbers, our arithmetic.' and 'Homes — prefilled from the report'. CAPTION: 'Homes: from the site report. Indicative.' VO: 'The homes number comes from the site report.'
3. 9–17s: type a Land price and a Sale price per home (tagged 'Illustrative numbers'). Profit and Margin on cost flip from '—' to figures. CAPTION: 'My numbers. Its arithmetic.' VO: 'I put in the land price and what they'd sell for.'
4. 17–23s: raise 'Build cost per m² — edit to your builder's rate' until Margin on cost turns red under 20%. CAPTION: 'Builder's real rate in. Margin goes red.' VO: 'Then my builder's actual rate. Ouch.'
5. 23–29s: the green box 'What you could pay for the land to keep a 20% margin:' held full-frame. CAPTION: 'That's the paddle limit.' VO: 'That's my limit. Over that, the paddle stays down.'
6. 29–35s: small-print card 'Rough: no GST, tax, holding costs or agent fees. Not advice. Run it past your builder, QS and bank.', then the end card. CAPTION: 'Run your numbers before the room does. Sign up at qsme.co.nz' VO: 'Run yours before the room does. Sign up at Q-S-me dot co dot en zed.'

## Risks
- Label every money figure 'Illustrative numbers'. The user supplies the land and sale prices.
- Not financial or investment advice. QSME doesn't value land or know sale prices. The defaults are editable starting points, not QSME cost data.
- Not a full feasibility: it leaves out GST, tax, holding period and sales commission. Say so on screen.
- Auckland only: the homes figure exists only where an Auckland zone profile exists.
- The filmed shot shows no faces, plates or real auction-house branding. The joke is auction FOMO, not bidders or agents.
