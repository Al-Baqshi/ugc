# Auckland first (we know): what you get when you sign up, council by council

**Situation:** A Christchurch couple and their mate in Dunedin see our Auckland flood-chip video and type their own addresses into QSME's 'Search your address'. They get less than the Aucklander did. Rather than let them find that out in the comments, we show exactly what each part of the country gets today, using three real public addresses.

**Truth:** Every 'nationwide' property tool has an asterisk, and people outside Auckland are used to finding it the hard way.

**Twist:** We read our own asterisk out loud. Auckland gets the lot. Christchurch and Hamilton get the zone. Dunedin gets boundary, land area, legal description and council, plus the product's own line: 'Auckland first, more councils to come'. The joke is on us: we started in Auckland, like everyone does.

## Beats
1. 0–3s HOOK: three-up split over the NZ outline. CAPTION: 'Not in Auckland? Here's exactly what you get.' VO: 'Straight answer for everyone outside Auckland.'
2. 3–11s: Auckland capture of a public or commercial address: boundary, land area, legal description, council, zone, then the services and hazard chips. CAPTION: 'Auckland: boundary, zone, services, hazards.' VO: 'Auckland gets the lot. Zone, pipes, flood and hazard checks.'
3. 11–18s: capture of a Christchurch (or Hamilton) civic building: boundary, land area, legal description, council and the zone name. CAPTION: 'Christchurch, Hamilton, Wellington City: + the zone.' VO: 'Christchurch, Hamilton and Wellington City get the zone too.'
4. 18–25s: capture of a Dunedin public building: boundary, land area, legal description, council. Zoom on 'Services & hazard data isn't available for this area yet — Auckland first, more councils to come.' CAPTION: 'Everywhere else: boundary, land area, legal description, council.' VO: 'Everywhere else, the boundary, the land area and the council. That's it, for now.'
5. 25–30s: kinetic self-own. The Auckland pin glows and the rest dim. CAPTION: 'We started in Auckland. We know.' VO: 'Yeah. We started in Auckland. We know.'
6. 30–34s CTA: end card. CAPTION: 'See what it knows about your section. Sign up at qsme.co.nz' VO: 'Sign up and type yours in. Q-S-me dot co dot en zed.'

## Hook layers
- **Text:** Not in Auckland? Here's exactly what you get.
- **Verbal:** Straight answer for everyone outside Auckland.
- **Visual:** Three phone-size captures side by side over a navy outline of NZ, pinned to Auckland, Christchurch and Dunedin. The Auckland card is visibly full of chips and the Dunedin one visibly short.
- **Audio:** Three 'plink' pin drops, each a note lower, then a deadpan beat of silence.

## Footage plan
- capture-public: Three homepage captures (mobile 1080x1920), each typing a public or commercial address: one in Auckland (e.g. 135 Albert Street), a Christchurch or Hamilton council or civic building, and a Dunedin public building. On the day, check that each returns the expected coverage (full / zone only / parcel and council) and log the addresses.
- kinetic: NZ outline map with three pins, the three-up split layout, captions, the dim/glow self-own beat and the end card, all in the motion kit.

**CTA:** Search your address — sign up at qsme.co.nz.

**Why now:** Every site-research video we post will draw 'does this work in Tauranga?' comments, and this is the honest answer to pin under them. It is made entirely from website captures, so it ships in week 1. It also retires the old 'any NZ address' line before traffic grows.

## Risks
- Check coverage live on capture day. If a council returns something different, change the captions, never the footage.
- Don't demo Wellington (inconsistent in live tests), even though it's listed for zoning.
- Never promise when more councils arrive. Show only the product's own 'more councils to come'.
- Don't voice the Site research headline 'Type any New Zealand address' as fact.
- The self-own is on QSME, never on Aucklanders or other regions.
