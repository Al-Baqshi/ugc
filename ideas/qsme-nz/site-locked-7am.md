# Turned up. Site's locked.

**Situation:** A plasterer drives 40 minutes across Auckland at 6:15am to a townhouse site in Hobsonville and finds the gate padlocked. It rained Tuesday, everything moved two days, and at 6:58am the builder's group chat says 'oh yeah we pushed you to Thursday'.

**Truth:** When a programme moves, the person who finds out last is the tradie standing at a locked gate.

**Twist:** QSME asks before it moves you: 'New dates — can you make them?' with 'Confirm dates / Can't make it'. The honest kicker: if he's not on QSME, the software tells the builder to 'Give them a ring.' Even the software says ring him.

## Beats
1. 0-3s HOOK: padlocked gate at dawn, van door slam, a chat bubble 'oh yeah we pushed you to Thurs' pops over it. Text: '6:58am. 40 mins in the van. Site's locked.' VO (Kiwi bloke, later): 'Forty minutes in the van. Gate's locked. And the group chat goes…'
2. 3-6s: caption: 'It rained Tuesday. Everything moved. Nobody told the plasterer.'
3. 6-12s: owner recording, web schedule: 'Rain day', tick Tuesday, the 'What moves' list, 'Push these tasks'. The chain slides, and the booked tasks show 'Waiting for their OK'.
4. 12-18s: the plasterer's iPhone (owner recording, second demo account): 'New dates to confirm', the amber card 'New dates — can you make them?', then 'Confirm dates'. Back on the chart the bar turns blue. VO: 'Asked. Not told.'
5. 18-22s: the other ending: 'Can't make it' with 'On another site until the 20th', and a red ring. Caption: 'Either way, nobody drives to a locked gate.'
6. 22-26s: honest kicker: a task with someone not on QSME shows 'Not on QSME — they won't be told about this task or asked to confirm. Give them a ring.' Caption: 'Even the software says ring him.' VO: 'And if he's not on it? It tells you to give him a ring. Fair.'
7. 26-29s: phone still of the project chat, 'Sweet as, see you Thursday.' End card: 'Your dates move with your OK. Join as a tradie — sign up at qsme.co.nz.'
