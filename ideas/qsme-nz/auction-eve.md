# The night before the auction

**Situation:** A first-time small developer has an Auckland section going to auction at 10am tomorrow. It's 9:40pm at the kitchen bench with the pre-approval letter on the fridge, and their 'budget' is a number they said out loud in the car. Nobody has run the numbers for the three homes they're dreaming of.

**Truth:** At NZ auctions people bid with their heart and do the maths afterwards. The room sets the price, not what the build actually stacks up to.

**Twist:** The calculator works backwards and gives you a walk-away number: 'What you could pay for the land to keep a 20% margin'. It's the mate who grabs your arm before you raise the paddle again.

## Risks
- Not financial advice, and no profit promise. Every figure on screen is the user's own input, labelled illustrative.
- The defaults ($4,000/m², $30k contributions, 15% soft costs, 8% finance) are editable starting points, not QSME cost data. Don't present them as market rates.
- It is not a valuation or a full feasibility study (no GST, tax, holding costs or commission).
- The homes estimate is Auckland only.
- Speaks to small developers more than ordinary home buyers, so shares will be modest.
