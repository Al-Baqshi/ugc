# Our whole contract is a thumbs-up

**Situation:** A Tauranga owner agreed $18,400 with a sparky for a rewire entirely over text. The thread ends 'Sweet as, see you Monday' and a thumbs-up emoji. Three weeks in, nobody can say whether the heat pump wiring was included.

**Truth:** Half the small jobs in NZ are agreed by text and sealed with a thumbs-up, because nobody wants to print, sign, scan and email.

**Twist:** The thumbs-up gets a deadpan legal reading ('Clause 1: thumbs-up'). Then QSME writes the thread up as a proper agreement and refuses to guess what the emoji meant: the heat pump line comes back as a [blank] for you to fill. The sparky then signs on his phone in the ute, straight from the link: no printer.

## Beats
1. 0-2.5s HOOK: a generic text thread (fictional names) ending 'Sweet as, see you Monday' plus a thumbs-up, zooming until the emoji fills the frame. Text: 'Our entire contract:'. VO (Mel, later): 'Here's our whole contract for the rewire.'
2. 2.5-7s: deadpan legal captions over the emoji: 'Clause 1: [thumbs-up]', 'Heat pump wiring included? [thumbs-up] (unclear)', 'Payment stages? [thumbs-up] (also unclear)'. VO: 'Clause one. Thumbs up. That's it. That's all the clauses.'
3. 7-10s: caption: 'Agreed in every sense. Except a signature.'
4. 10-16s: owner recording, Agreements: paste the thread, choose 'Write it up as a proper agreement', and the names and $18,400 come back while the heat pump wiring stays a [blank]. Zoom on: 'AI never makes up an amount, a date or a name. Anything it was not given stays a [blank] for you.' VO: 'It won't guess what the thumbs-up meant. You fill the blank.'
5. 16-22s: owner recording on a phone browser, the sparky's side (test signer): open the link, 'I have read this agreement and I agree to sign it electronically.', type the name, draw a finger signature, then 'Signed. Download a copy below and keep it.'
6. 22-25s: the sender's list flips to 'Everyone has signed', with a quick flash of the certificate page.
7. 25-28s: end card: 'Signed from a link. No printer.' / 'Get it signed — sign up at qsme.co.nz'

## Risks
- Templates and AI write-ups are a starting point, not legal advice. Never 'legally binding everywhere' or 'lawyer-approved'.
- The AI write-up uses one credit. No plan, credit or pricing lines in content beyond the small 'uses a credit' note.
- Signing happens in the phone's browser, not inside the iOS app.
- Don't state the $30k written-contract rule unless it's verified. This job is under it anyway.
- The joke is the emoji, never the sparky or the owner.
