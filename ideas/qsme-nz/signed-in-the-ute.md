# Signed in the ute

**Situation:** A small Auckland builder-developer awarded the plastering package three weeks ago, over the phone ('yeah, sweet, start Monday'), and the crew is already on site. Nothing has been signed, and QSME's Agreements page has shown an amber banner about it all week.

**Truth:** Half the jobs in NZ start with a phone call and a 'sweet as', and the paperwork is always something you'll do this arvo.

**Twist:** The software nags you, politely: '2 awarded jobs have nothing signed for them'. Then 'Make the agreement' writes it from the job itself, and the plasterer signs in his ute with his finger, straight from the link. 'Signed. Download a copy below and keep it.' Done before his pie goes cold.

## Beats
1. 0–3s HOOK. CAPTION: 'Awarded 3 weeks ago. Signed: never.' VO: 'Plasterer's been on site three weeks. Never signed a thing.'
2. 3–10s: 'Make the agreement', and the agreement appears with the agreed sum and its GST wording, dates, retention, scope and payment stages, with both parties on the signing line. CAPTION: 'Written from the job itself.' VO: 'One button writes it from the job. Sum, dates, retention, stages.'
3. 10–15s: 'A draft — check it and send it', then 'Send for signing'. CAPTION: 'A draft. You check it. You send it.' VO: 'It's a draft. I check it, I send it.'
4. 15–23s: owner-filmed phone in a ute, hand only. The link opens in the phone browser. Tick 'I have read this agreement and I agree to sign it electronically.', type a fictional name under 'Your full name', draw a finger signature, and 'Signed. Download a copy below and keep it.' appears. CAPTION: 'One link. One finger. Signed in the ute.' VO: 'He signs on his phone, straight from the link.'
5. 23–28s: the sender's list moves from 'Out for signing' to 'Everyone has signed', then flips to the certificate page. CAPTION: 'Signed copy and certificate, kept with the job.' VO: 'Signed copy's filed with the job.'
6. 28–33s CTA: end card. CAPTION: 'Get it signed before the pie goes cold. Sign up at qsme.co.nz', with a small line: 'Templates are a starting point, not legal advice.' VO: 'Get it signed. Sign up at Q-S-me dot co dot en zed.'

## Hook layers
- **Text:** Awarded 3 weeks ago. Signed: never.
- **Verbal:** Plasterer's been on site three weeks. We never signed anything.
- **Visual:** Owner screen recording of the amber banner '2 awarded jobs have nothing signed for them', with a red marker circling 'nothing signed'.
- **Audio:** One soft reminder ping, then an awkward beat of silence.

**CTA:** Get the job signed from a link. Sign up at qsme.co.nz.

**Why now:** Agreements and 'Make the agreement' shipped 21–23 Sep, and spring is when the most jobs start on a handshake.

## Risks
- Nothing is written or sent automatically at award, so always show the draft-and-check step.
- Templates are NZ-law starting points, not legal advice. Never say 'legally binding everywhere' or 'court-proof'.
- Signing happens in the phone's browser, not inside the iPhone app. Don't show the app for this.
- No placed signature boxes, witnesses or signing order, on screen or in the VO.
- Fictional names only. The joke is handshake culture, never the plasterer.
