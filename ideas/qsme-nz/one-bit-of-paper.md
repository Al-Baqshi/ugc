# Everything's finished. Except one bit of paper.

**Situation:** An owner-builder in Hamilton has finished the house (painted, carpets down, family desperate to move in before Christmas), but the CCC application is stuck on one missing Record of Work from the roofer. He's now on a job in Queenstown and answers texts on Sundays.

**Truth:** The last 1% of a build is paperwork, and the CCC waits on the one certificate nobody chased while the tradie was still on site.

**Twist:** A certificate hunt that escalates: inbox search 'record of work' gives 'No results', then the garage boxes, then the ute glovebox turns up a mince-and-cheese pie receipt. The fix isn't a better search. It's the certificate going in while they're still on site, and the pack downloading as one zip.
