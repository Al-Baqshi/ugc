# The Facebook group

**Truth:** Finding a builder in NZ means posting in a local Facebook group and wading through hundreds of comments of cousins, mates and warnings.

**Twist:** Escalation: the comments pile up until 'Dave' has 9 separate threads (recommended, warned against, and Dave himself).

## Beats
1. Hook: generic group post 'Anyone recommend a builder in Hamilton?' — text '212 comments. Still no builder.' + notification ping
2. Comments scroll: 'my cousin Dave', 'DON'T use Dave', 'Dave here, flick me a DM', 'following', 'following'
3. Escalate: Dave count ticks up to 9 threads
4. Payoff: 'Or. Put the actual job on QSME.' Send one job to the trades you choose; every one prices the same lines (no 'verified' or licence claims, per the truth sheet)
5. CTA: 'Put the actual job on QSME. Sign up at qsme.co.nz'

## Hook layers
Text: '212 comments. Still no builder.' / Visual: endless comment scroll / Audio: notification ping spam

## Tools (suggested)
Mock UI built as HTML/Figma and screen-recorded; ElevenLabs VO optional; CapCut edit

## Notes
Use a GENERIC group UI, not Facebook's real branding. Comment names are fictional. Humor aims at the process, not at tradies.
