# Section to shovel: one Auckland address, start to finish

**Situation:** A first-time small developer has an Auckland section in mind and wants answers in order: what is it zoned, what could go there, what would it need, do the numbers work, and what happens next. They want this before paying a planner just to learn the basics. The founder (a QS) works through one demo Auckland address on screen.

**Truth:** First-time developers don't know what they don't know, and the order of work is the hard part.

**Twist:** The honest kind: software that keeps telling you when to call a professional. The video stops at every 'Confirm with a planner or the council' and says exactly what to ask them.

## Beats
1. 0:00-0:30 COLD OPEN: the map flies to a demo Auckland parcel. Founder line (on camera or VO): 'I'm a QS. Let's take one Auckland section from what's it zoned to a priced, tendered, programmed job, and I'll show you where you still need a planner.'
2. 0:30-1:30 CH1 THE FRONT DOOR: the homepage address search (boundary, land area, legal description, council, zone, the nine Auckland chips). Honest aside: outside the four zoning councils you get the boundary and council only.
3. 1:30-3:00 CH2 WHAT COULD GO HERE: Site research covers the zone in plain words ('Without resource consent / With a resource consent') and the standards grid, then 'Planning overlays & controls'. The Pro view shows its layers (Zoning, Buildings, Services, Flood & hazards) and the Title tab's owner count, with the CV & sales pill left locked.
4. 3:00-4:15 CH3 WHAT IT WOULD TAKE: the Development potential verdict, then 'What this site would require' with the Required filter. The founder flags each row that means 'call a planner / surveyor / order the LIM'.
5. 4:15-5:15 CH4 DO THE NUMBERS WORK: Rough feasibility with illustrative numbers and the 20%-margin land figure, then the 'One pager' print preview to hand a builder or partner.
6. 5:15-6:30 CH5 FROM LOOKING TO DOING: 'Create project here', then an SOQ from a template, then a private tender cut from a pocket. The bidder's view shows the same lines with no rates.
7. 6:30-7:30 CH6 WHEN: 'Standard NZ build — simple flow' laid out from a start date. NZ public holidays and the Christmas shutdown move the bars, and 'What drives the finish date' rings the chain. Close: 'Indicative only. Call the planner — now you know what to ask.' CTA.

## Risks
- A long owner recording session (weeks 3-4). Screens may date when the shorter sidebar on a branch ships.
- Keep the CV & sales tab locked or cut around it, and never mock values into it.
- Template durations are estimates. Never 'your build will take X months'.
- Tender with invited demo contacts only. Don't show the near-empty public board or imply market scale.
- If an AI draft SOQ is shown, it must be visibly checked by a person. No 'compared line by line'.
- Every disclaimer card stays on screen long enough to read.
