# Room for 3!* (STCA)

**Situation:** A couple section-hunting in Auckland on a Sunday with a flat white each. The next listing's flyer shouts 'Room for 3!* STCA'. They've got 40 minutes before the open home and no idea what 'subject to council approval' actually involves.

**Truth:** In NZ property, four small letters (STCA) carry the whole dream. Everyone who has browsed sections has seen them, and nobody knows what they really cost.

**Twist:** QSME unpacks the asterisk. The '*' prints out like a long receipt that becomes 'What this site would require': a stack of red REQUIRED badges, each with a 'because:'. Even the zone line agrees '…up to three…' but adds 'that meet the zone standards'. The joke is on the phrase, not the agent.

## Beats
1. 0-2.5s HOOK: fictional flyer 'ROOM FOR 3!*' (no agency, no address), zoom into the asterisk. Receipt-printer whirr.
2. 2.5-5s: the footnote slides up: '*STCA = subject to council approval'. Caption: 'Four letters. Doing a lot of lifting.' VO (Mel, later): 'Room for three. Asterisk. S-T-C-A.'
3. 5-10s: owner recording, Site research on a demo Auckland Mixed Housing Suburban address: 'What could go here' then 'Up to three dwellings per site that meet the zone standards'. A marker underlines 'that meet the zone standards'.
4. 10-14s: the 'Development potential' pill pops in, plus and minus chips cascade, and the 'Homes with consent — indicative range' tile counts up (real values from the recording).
5. 14-21s: the asterisk 'prints' into 'What this site would require'. Red REQUIRED badges stack, zoom on two 'because:' lines, tap 'Required' and the list collapses to the must-haves. VO: 'That's the asterisk. Itemised.'
6. 21-25s: the grey disclaimer is held on screen: 'Indicative only — … Confirm with a planner or the council.' Caption: 'Not a promise. Your list of questions.'
7. 25-28s: end card: 'Unpack the asterisk before you fall for the section.' / 'Sign up at qsme.co.nz · full picture Auckland first'. VO: 'Sign up at Q-S-me dot co dot en zed.'

## Risks
- The humour must land on the phrase 'STCA', never on agents. Agents are potential customers through My Listings.
- Never say 'you can build three' or that consent will be granted. Always say 'indicatively'.
- Yield numbers are Auckland only. Zone profiles are hand-written from the Unitary Plan and plan changes can outdate them, so keep the disclaimer on screen.
- Which requirement rows appear depends on the address. Pick a demo site that returns several REQUIRED rows with clear 'because:' lines.
- Needs an owner recording (week 2+).
