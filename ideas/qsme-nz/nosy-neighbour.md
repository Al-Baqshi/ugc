# Nosy neighbour

**Truth:** Everyone has a fenced-off section on their street they've been wondering about for months.

**Twist:** Nosiness framed as a feature: 'You can actually see what they're building.'

## Beats
1. Hook: 'You know that fenced-off section down your road that's been sitting there 8 months?'
2. Search address → live projects map
3. Show what a live project looks like (stages, timeline)
4. Turn: 'Building yourself? Yours can be on here too.'
5. CTA: 'Search your address — sign up at qsme.co.nz'

## Hook layers
Verbal call-out + visual of fenced section + text 'what are they building??'

## Tools (suggested)
Real screen recording of the map; B-roll of a fenced section (real or Runway/Veo)

## Notes
⚠️ Confirm exactly what the public map shows before promising it. Privacy: don't show real addresses/owners without permission.
