# Price it once: from the consent pack to one scope every trade prices

**Situation:** A Christchurch owner-builder has a consent pack thick enough to prop a door open and a spreadsheet from her brother-in-law's build two years ago. She needs a budget she trusts, and quotes from trades that are pricing the same job, before the drainlayer prices from a pack with no drainage sheet in it.

**Truth:** Everyone prices a build from someone else's spreadsheet and a consent pack nobody has read cover to cover.

**Twist:** The best moment isn't a number, it's a gap: 'Heads up: no drainage found in this pack', found before the tender, not on site. And the estimate isn't the end of the job, it's the thing you tender. Every bidder prices the same lines and never sees your rates.

## Risks
- 'Line by line' in the title means pricing the SOQ. Never say or caption that bids are compared line by line.
- The AI reads what the drawings say; it doesn't measure or do takeoff. Mark-up is switched off, so no 'Open drawing' buttons on screen.
- Plan reading and the AI use credits on paid plans. Never call them free, and don't advertise the 14-day trial.
- Keep the Beta tag and 'It can get things wrong' visible whenever the chat is on screen.
- The plan set must be a sample or one the owner has permission to show; architects' title blocks are someone's IP.
- QSME doesn't supply rates. AI estimates aren't quotes or market rates.
