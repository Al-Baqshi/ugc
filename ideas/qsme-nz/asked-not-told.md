# Asked, not told

**Situation:** 6:45am Tuesday in Auckland: a plumber pulls up to a locked site gate. The framing ran three days late, the programme was re-jigged in a spreadsheet on Friday night, and nobody told him. Now his whole week is shuffled.

**Truth:** Tradies find out their dates moved when they turn up to a locked gate.

**Twist:** Status flip: in QSME the plumber gets a vote. Move a booked task and it's proposed, not moved: 'nothing changes for them until they say yes'. 'Can't make it' is a real button with a reason box, so the PM gets 'On another site until the 20th' instead of a no-show.

## Beats
1. 0–3s HOOK (filmed gate). CAPTION: 'POV: the plumber. 6:45am. Gate's locked.' VO: 'Six forty-five. Gate's locked. Framing's three days behind. Nobody told me.'
2. 3–9s: PM side. Drag Framing three days right; the linked bars slide and the toast reads '3 moved · 2 sent for OK — 5 that follow them came too · Undo'. CAPTION: 'Framing slips. Everything linked after it moves…' VO: 'On Q-S-me, when framing slips, everything after it moves…'
3. 9–15s: the plumber's task stays as a faded agreed bar plus a dashed proposed bar with an amber ring. 'This needs [name]'s OK — nothing changes for them until they say yes.', then 'Send for their OK'. CAPTION: '…except the people booked. They get asked.' VO: '…except the people booked on it. They get asked.'
4. 15–22s: tradie side. 'New dates to confirm', then the amber card 'New dates — can you make them?', then 'Can't make it' with the typed reason 'On another site until the 20th.' CAPTION: 'And he's allowed to say no.' VO: 'And you're allowed to say no.'
5. 22–27s: PM chart. A red 'Can't make it' ring; the PM picks the 21st; the tradie taps 'Confirm dates' and the bar turns blue. CAPTION: 'Pick a day that works. Confirmed. Blue.' VO: 'They pick a day that works. Confirmed.'
6. 27–31s CTA: end card. CAPTION: 'Asked, not told. Join as a tradie: sign up at qsme.co.nz', with a small line: 'In-app and email.' VO: 'Asked, not told. Sign up at Q-S-me dot co dot en zed.'

**CTA:** Tradies: get asked, not told. Sign up at qsme.co.nz.

**Why now:** None of our produced videos speak from the tradie's side yet, and spring is when programmes slip most. 'Asked, not told' works on web and email today.

## Risks
- No SMS (delivery is failing) and no Android. Say 'in-app and email', and don't show push notifications unless they're confirmed.
- Don't show the native iPhone schedule screens (merged, not confirmed in the store build). Use mobile web or the email.
- People not on QSME aren't messaged ('Give them a ring'), so don't imply every tradie is reached.
- The POV voice is a fictional character, not a customer testimonial. Don't caption it as a real plumber.
- Never say the dates are 'locked' or 'legally agreed'. The punch is the spreadsheet, not the PM.
