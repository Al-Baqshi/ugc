# 5 things you can't see from the footpath

**Situation:** Saturday section-hunting in Auckland: you're on the footpath looking at a tidy section with a For Sale sign, and it looks completely ordinary. Under it, over it and around it are rules you can't see from the street.

**Truth:** What kills a build plan is invisible from the footpath: a public pipe, a protected view of the maunga, old-house rules, a road that wants a strip, and where the water runs.

**Twist:** The video 'X-rays' an ordinary section with marker overlays (a red pipe, a sight-line cone, a 'pre-1944' stamp, a dashed road line, blue water arrows), and each one resolves into QSME's real line for it. The villa has better protection than you do.
