# Which one's cheapest?

**Situation:** Sunday night at a Hamilton kitchen bench: a homeowner has three quotes for a bathroom. One says '$8,298 incl. GST', one says '$7,400 + GST', and a sole trader who isn't GST-registered wrote a flat '$7,900'. Their partner asks the only question that matters: which one's cheapest?

**Truth:** The quote that looks cheapest is usually the one that wrote '+ GST' in small letters.

**Twist:** The $7,400 quote is never the cheapest, on either basis ($8,510 incl.). Flip 'Compare on' from Excl. to Incl. GST and the 'Lowest' tag jumps from one of the other bidders to the other, while the one that looked cheapest never wins. QSME doesn't pick the winner. It just stops GST picking it for you.

## Risks
- Never say 'line by line', 'apples to apples' or anything implying a side-by-side line matrix. It's a totals table, and each bid opens to its own lines.
- QSME doesn't recommend a bid, judge 'conforming' or correct prices. The owner decides.
- Emailed quotes are read into a draft the owner checks. Never say 'imported automatically'. Outlook .msg isn't supported.
- Demo numbers and fictional bidders only, labelled 'Demo bids'.
- Update the existing apples-oranges-pineapple card rather than logging a duplicate.
