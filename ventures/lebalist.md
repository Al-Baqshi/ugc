# Lebalist

_Created 2026-11-14 from the product repo (`~/Dev/ship-lebalist`: docs/lebalist-overview.md, docs/00-north-star.md, app i18n, launch memory) and the live site. ❓ = still to confirm with the owner._

## One-liner
Lebanon's everything-marketplace and community app: buy, sell, rent, hire, book and chat with people near you, in Arabic and English. https://lebalist.com · "Lebanon" + "list". Core promise (product docs): *Everything Lebanon buys, sells, and books, in one place.*

## Audience
- People in Lebanon who buy, sell and look for services: someone selling a used phone or fridge, someone hunting for an apartment or a car, tradespeople (plumbers, electricians, tilers…), small shops, restaurants, professionals. ❓ Which slice comes first?
- Language: Lebanese Arabic in speech; reading is Arabic and English, and French slips into everyday talk. ❓ Is the Lebanese diaspora (Gulf, Europe, Africa, Australia) a target too?
- Where they scroll: ❓ (likely Instagram Reels, TikTok, WhatsApp Status, Facebook).
- What they already believe: buying and selling happens in Facebook groups and WhatsApp groups, and you find a tradesperson through "someone who knows someone".

## Pains (specific, in their words) ❓ to confirm by ear
- «خاص» / «إنبوكس»: dozens of comments on a post and nobody buys.
- «بكم؟» when the price is already written in the post. «آخر سعر؟»
- «بعرفلك واحد»: a tradesperson's number forwarded through three relatives, and it's out of date.
- Addresses given as landmarks («حدّ الفرن، بعد الدكانة…») because streets have no names or numbers.
- Global apps don't fit: card checkout, apps that don't know the areas.

## The payoff
One place for Lebanon to trade and get things done, sorted by your own area, in your language.

## Value props (from the product docs; check what is live before claiming)
- 10 categories in one app: real estate, cars, marketplace, electronics, food, construction trades, professional services, notary directory, jobs, on-demand transport.
- Local by design: listings sorted by governorate → district → area, so "near me" means near you. Map browsing.
- Chat and calls in the app (the north star: "chat is the reason to open it daily").
- Verified providers for services; ratings and reviews.
- Booking without double-booking (rentals, appointments). Live selling and a community feed.
- Arabic with full right-to-left support, plus English.
- Sign-up with a username and password, recovery codes, passkeys; email and phone optional. ❓ See the pricing rule below before using any of this in content.
- Prices in USD. Payments (when switched on) through OMT, Whish, bank transfer or cash; no card needed.

## Storytelling rule (owner, 2026-11-14)
- **Story first, product between the lines.** No "we're here, we're the solution" scripts: the owner called that naive. Write a story people enjoy watching for itself; the viewer works out that Lebalist is the answer. Lebalist is not explained in the voice-over (no "open Lebalist, pick your area…"); at most a visual clue and the end card.
- Ending (owner 2026-11-14 on LB01): one short spoken line «نزّل ليبالِست (من هون)» (Download Lebalist here) over the logo + store badges. Only publish "download" videos once the app is in the stores.
