## CTA rule (owner, 2026-10-03) — overrides everything older
- The goal is **sign-ups**. Every CTA pushes people to create an account: "Sign up at qsme.co.nz", "Create your account at qsme.co.nz", "Get started at qsme.co.nz", "Search your address — sign up at qsme.co.nz".
- **Never say in content:** "free", "free to join", "free check", "no sign-up", "no login", "without an account", "no listing fee", "your own crew is always free", "bid free", "no fees", success-fee details, or any other generous pricing line. Don't describe what you get without signing up.
- Site search / site research: present it as something you get **when you sign up** (the full Site Research needs an account). Awareness is good; giving it away is not.

## One-liner
One place for NZ developers and property owners to plan, price and deliver a build, from feasibility to Code Compliance (CCC). Free to join.

## Audience
Two-sided marketplace:
- **Owners / small developers**: building a home, subdividing or renovating. ❓ age, first-timers vs repeat developers
- **Tradies & consultants**: builders, sparkies, plumbers, roofers, QS, architects, engineers, inspectors. Want to win work.
- Where they scroll: ❓ (guess: FB/IG/TikTok for owners, FB/TikTok for tradies)
- Market: Aotearoa, NZ English

## Pains (from site copy + NZ reality)
- Quotes that price different assumptions, so they can't be compared ("apples to apples")
- Finding tradies through Facebook groups and word of mouth
- Documents living in someone's inbox; the CCC/consent paper trail
- Not knowing the numbers before committing to a section or build
- Losing track of stages, inspections and payment claims

## The payoff
Calm and control: you know what it costs, who's doing it, where it's at, and every document is there when the council, the bank or a buyer asks.

## Value props
- AI feasibility & estimates from drawings → numbers before you commit
- Tenders cut from one priced schedule → every bidder prices the same lines; bids compared as totals excl. and incl. GST with a "Lowest" tag (each bid opens to its own lines). ⚠️ Don't say "compared line by line": that matrix is branch-only (fact-check 2026-10-02).
- Profiles with a track record and two-way ratings → better than "my cousin's mate from the FB group". ⚠️ Don't claim "verified tradies": licence verification isn't confirmed live and 1 of 22 profiles is verified (fact-check 2026-10-02).
- Site research: type an address → zone, what could go here, overlays, services & hazards, development potential, rough feasibility, one-page site report. ⚠️ Zoning: Auckland, Wellington City, Christchurch, Hamilton only. Overlays/services/hazards/verdict: Auckland only.
- One timeline for stages, inspections, claims → see the build move
- Plans that answer questions, citing the page
- Works with Claude / ChatGPT via MCP
- (internal) Free to start — never used in content
