# Consistent CEO

## One-liner
A self-hosted AI operator for people who run several things at once: give it a mission in plain words, it plans and does the work on your own computer, asks before anything leaves it, and shows proof of what changed.

## Audience (owner 2026-10-13: founders & operators)
- Solo founders, operators and agencies running several businesses, projects and goals at once.
- Where they scroll: LinkedIn, X, YouTube ❓
- What they already believe: AI assistants say "done" too easily; their data shouldn't live on someone else's server ❓

## Pains (in their words) ❓ to confirm
- "I'm running six things and finishing none of them."
- "The AI said it was done. It wasn't."

## The payoff
Everything you run keeps moving, even when you're not looking, and nothing goes out without your yes.

## Value props (from the repo, /Users/brave/Dev/Consistent-world — README, docs/VISION.md, STATUS.md)
- Missions in plain words → planned and worked on your own computer (entities, missions, tasks, heartbeats).
- Runs locally: desktop app for Mac only today (v0.2.0, signed DMGs, Apple silicon + Intel, released 2026-10-12); Windows/Linux builds and the Docker image not published yet. Starts at login, keeps going when the window is closed (menu bar).
- Any brain: your Claude or ChatGPT subscription via their CLIs, a local Ollama model, or an API key.
- Policy & approval gate: anything outward-facing waits for your approval.
- Evidence packs: git-diff proof that a run actually changed something.
- Phone app: NOT installable yet (early access, not in App Store/Play; push off by default). Don't show or claim phone approvals.

## Do NOT claim (yet) — fact-checked 2026-10-13 against the repo + www.consistent.ceo
- "Shows a diff / passing test as proof": evidence packs are git change counts; the app flags 'Unverified' when a run says done but nothing changed. Desktop shows no evidence pack.
- "Nothing leaves without your yes": outward actions are flagged for approval by the AI planner; in AutoRun some entity actions can send without approval.
- Windows, Docker, phone app availability.
- Visible evidence states on every claim (Verified/Observed/Inferred): shadow mode only, users don't see them (STATUS.md).
- App Store / Play Store availability (not submitted).
