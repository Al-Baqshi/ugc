## One-liner
Tendering and estimating software for construction organisations: schedule of quantities → private tenders (every bidder prices the same lines) → bids compared as totals excl./incl. tax → award becomes a contract + e-sign from a link → Gantt, daily log, QR sign-in. One record per project.

## Audience
- Main contractors (subcontract packages)
- Quantity surveyors & project managers running tenders for clients
- Clients / developers / asset owners
- Enterprise / white-label
- Where they scroll: LinkedIn first, then YouTube/X ❓
- Markets: outside NZ ❓ (AU, UK, Middle East?)

## Pains
- Scope never itemised → bidders price different assumptions
- Reformatting quotes from different spreadsheets/PDFs into one comparison
- Bids on different tax bases, hidden exclusions
- Contract doesn't match the winning bid
- Office and site working from different facts
- Nobody knows who was on site on a given day

## The payoff
What was priced = what was agreed = what was built. No reconciliation nightmares, and an audit trail that builds itself.

## Value props
- Estimate priced once, every tender cut from it → identical lines
- Rates and budget never shown to bidders
- Award creates contract, milestones, retention, e-sign
- Gantt filled from the estimate, critical path
- QR gate sign-in + daily log with photos
- iOS app, MCP/AI connectors

## Real proof we can use
❓ NZ production usage numbers, customer logos/quotes, time saved per tender
