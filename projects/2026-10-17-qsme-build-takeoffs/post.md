Title: Measured Once, Typed Zero Times: Takeoffs in QSME
Description:
Measured once. Typed three times. Guess which one's wrong.

In QSME, takeoffs start on the drawing: PDF, DWG or IFC. Point at a line for its run, inside a room for its area, box one downlight and it counts every one drawn like it. Tick every bedroom, every window by its tag.

Add it to the SOQ and the line stays linked: re-measure and it follows, tap the ruler and you're back on the drawing it came from.

Client? Each trade gets a draft tender with just its pages and its SOQ jobs. Main contractor? Take off your own job, price it from your rate book, and start each contract from the SOQ jobs. And when Rev C lands, measurements carry over and what changed gets flagged.

Book a 30-minute demo: https://qsme.build
