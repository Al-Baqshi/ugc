# VO script: Kiwi performance

**Character:** Mel: everyday Kiwi woman, late 30s, a first-time small developer with her partner (the 'we'). The same Mel as 'Which Dave?', so the NZ series sounds like one person. General NZ accent, never broad or a caricature, not Australian. Dry, under-played, amused at herself: the joke is her own auction FOMO, never bidders, agents or auction houses. She talks to a mate, not to camera; product moments sound like the same person, no announcer lift, no tagline cadence. AI voice, never presented as a real customer or testimonial.
