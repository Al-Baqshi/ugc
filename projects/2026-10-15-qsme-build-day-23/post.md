# Day 23 of one signature

**The joke in one sentence:** A big mono counter says the ceilings subcontract has been out for 23 days. Every reply was innocent and true ("Will sign tonight 👍", "Went to junk", "Printer's out of toner", "Signed and scanned!"), and then the scan arrives sideways, because the scanner skipped exactly one page: page 7, retention. The crew has been on site since Day 2.

**The insight:** The subcontract is the one document on the job that everybody agrees with and nobody has signed. The friction isn't bad faith. It's the print, sign, scan and email loop. In QSME you bring in the subcontract PDF you already use, choose "Sign it as it is", and the subcontractor signs from one link on their phone, in the browser, with nothing to install. The signed copy comes back with every page you sent, page 7 included, plus a certificate page.

**Job:** This is organic week 2. It closes the week's arc (compare → award → sign) on Thursday morning in the UK and the Gulf, when signatures get chased before the weekend. It goes out from the founder's LinkedIn profile, the QSME page reposts it, and the 9:16 cut goes to YouTube Shorts in the same slot. It continues the plasterboard & ceilings package from "Six bids. Six formats." and "The Transposition": the awarded ceilings subcontractor is now on site and unsigned. The subcontractor is never the butt. Junk folders and toner happen to everyone, the on-screen caption blames the scanner for page 7, and the close turns the joke on us ("Still can't fix junk folders.").

## Music

- **Direction:** There's no music under the skit (0–14.25). The rhythm comes from the counter: soft mechanical odometer ticks, generic message pops and a date-stamp thump on every reply, then a single phone buzz for Day 23 and the marker squeak on the missing page. The deadpan needs that dry air. Music enters on the QSME logo at 14.25 as a light, tidy pulse that feels like paperwork finally moving. It lifts slightly through the "Signed." chime and the status flip (18.7–20.45), ducks under the last four VO lines and resolves on one chord at about 28.3.
- **Mood / tempo:** Dry, understated, quietly satisfied. 92–100 BPM (suggest 96). Muted plucks, a soft kick, a warm low pad and maybe one light shaker. No risers, no "corporate ukulele", no trending track.
- **Source:** ElevenLabs Music on a plan that includes commercial use. Prompt: "minimal muted plucked synth and soft kick, 96 BPM, understated and tidy, warm low pad, light shaker, no vocals, no risers, 14.5 seconds, resolves on a single held chord at 14.0s". Or use a stock library track licensed for business/brand channels that also covers paid social. SFX come from ElevenLabs Sound Effects (odometer ticks, rewind whirr, stamp thumps, phone buzz, scanner whirr, marker, taps, scribble, chime). Nothing that imitates Outlook, WhatsApp, iMessage, DocuSign or OS sounds.
- **Licensing:** LinkedIn has no licensed music library, and a later paid cut needs commercial rights, so use generated or commercially licensed audio only. No CapCut library audio, templates or AI voices.
