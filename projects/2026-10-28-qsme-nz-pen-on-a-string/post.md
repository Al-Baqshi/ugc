# The pen on a string

**The joke in one sentence:** The most important record on site hangs off a gate on a plastic sleeve and a pen on a string, and the one time someone asks who was on site Thursday, the pen's gone and Thursday says "D—".

**How it's told:** an object documentary. A lost-pet-style MISSING poster for the site pen opens it ("Pictured: happier times."), the soggy sign-in book is the evidence, and the fix is QSME's own instruction, read straight: "Print it, laminate it, cable-tie it to the fence." The callback runs twice: "Still no pen." after the second sign-in, and a cardboard tag on the empty string at the very end, "PEN: STILL MISSING". The humour stays on the pen, the rain and the paper book, never on the tradies who signed it.

**Job:** organic. Week 4's funny short for builders and site managers, posted Wednesday 6:00 am while they're scrolling before the gate opens. Every CTA pushes sign-ups (owner rule 2026-10-03): end card button and VO "Sign up at qsme.co.nz".

**What changed from the calendar brief (and why):**
- **CTA.** The end card is "One poster on the gate." + **Sign up at qsme.co.nz**, not a bare URL, and the brief's 16–19 s caption "No app. No account. No pen." is now **"Still no pen."** "No account" is banned by the owner CTA rule, and the callback is funnier anyway.
- **No "one tap".** In the live public page the name, phone and company are remembered on that phone ("Welcome back, {name} · Not you?"), but the PPE lines and the agreement have to be ticked again every day. So the caption says "Next morning, it remembers you." and we show the ticks (marked "▸▸ sped up").
- **"Today on this site" is the worker's screen.** In the live bundle that heading sits on the signed-in confirmation and lists the day's tasks. The builder's page is **On site**: "Signed in · today" (a table sorted by company, with a "walked in" tag), "Due today, not signed in yet", and Print ▾ → "The register as a PDF, on your letterhead". The video shows each where it really lives.
- **Length 29.6 s** (brief 26 s, +14%, inside ±15%). The extra time goes to reading time where the sound-off viewer needs it: the product's own instructions build line by line (3.6 s), the sign-in page holds on the builder's logo and site before the sped-up fill, "Signed in. On the record." and "Still no pen." get a beat each, and the builder's side (the payoff for builders and site managers) has three readable beats: "“D—” now has a surname." 1.9 s, "And who's still due." 1.7 s, the register 2.25 s.
- **The two QSME days are Wed 28 and Thu 29 Oct.** The builder's page reads "Today · Thu, 29 Oct" with Dave Kettle signed in at 7:14 am, so "Who was on site Thursday?" gets an answer with a full name and a time, not just a pun.
- **Christchurch stays in the story, not on screen as an address.** The demo project is "Two townhouses (demo), Christchurch", with no street, so no private address ever shows.

## Music and sound direction

- **Shape:** 0–6.6 s plays like a missing-person appeal: a single low cello note and a sparse, slightly mournful piano figure under real rain, deadpan-serious (the joke is treating a biro like a lost dog). The snip at 6.85 is the turn: the cue drops out for half a beat, then a light, dry, mid-tempo groove comes in with the new poster (about 100 BPM, muted acoustic guitar, upright bass, brushed snare, a little glockenspiel on the success chimes), sitting low under the phone and desk beats. It resolves on one held chord as the PEN tag swings in (27.95) and the rain is left alone for the last second.
- **Source (commercial use only):** ElevenLabs Music on a plan that permits commercial use, prompts: (A) *"sombre investigative underscore, solo cello drone and sparse felt piano, 70 BPM, missing-person documentary feel played completely straight, 7 seconds, ends on an unresolved note"*; (B) *"light dry upbeat groove, muted acoustic guitar, upright bass, brushed snare, glockenspiel accents, 100 BPM, no vocals, practical and a bit cheeky, 23 seconds, resolves on a held chord"*. Or a business-tier Epidemic Sound / Artlist licence. Never in-app music libraries for the master. Keep prompts and licence receipts in `outputs/licences/`.
- **SFX (ElevenLabs SFX or licensed library):** rain on corrugated iron, plastic sleeve crinkle, single drip, side-cutter snip, paper flap/fall, four cable-tie zips, laminate shimmer, soft generic UI taps (never the iOS keyboard or camera sounds), a small success chime, paper/printer slide, cardboard tag creak. Exact times are in `vo-script.json` → post_processing.
- **Mix:** VO 6–8 LU above the music; rain bed never drops to digital silence; reel at about -14 LUFS, -1 dBTP.
