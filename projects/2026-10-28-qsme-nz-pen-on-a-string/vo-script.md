Talks like he's telling the smoko room, not like a narrator: low, flat, bone-dry, a little resigned about the pen and quietly pleased about the poster. General NZ accent (short centralised 'i' in 'pen', 'string', 'sign'; a relaxed 'Thurs-dee'), never a caricature, never Australian, no announcer lift, no exclamation marks, every line falls at the end. **Model & settings:** `eleven_multilingual_v2` · stability 0.5 · similarity 0.75 · style 0 · speed 1 · speaker boost on

**Video:** `outputs/pen-on-a-string-9x16.mp4` (29.6 s, silent master with every caption burned in; times below are seconds on this file).

**Casting line:** “Pen on a string lasts about a week on our site. Then it's just string, eh.”

Owner CTA rule (2026-10-03): no “free”, “no account”, “no app” or pricing line in any take.

## Post-processing

- Time base: every time is seconds on outputs/pen-on-a-string-9x16.mp4 (29.6 s, silent master, captions burned in). Lay the stems on with tools/mux.
- Generation (v2 default): one request per line with request stitching (previous_text = the line before, next_text = the line after; leave next_text empty for vo08 and vo13 so they end final). Fixed seed per take, 3 takes per request, pick by ear. Use the continuous read as the casting and consistency reference; v3 only as a fallback, as one continuous request sliced afterwards.
- Never time-stretch more than about 4%. If a take runs past max_s, use another take. Trim digital silence; keep a quiet inhale before vo02, vo03 and vo12 (pulled down 6 to 10 dB).
- Room tone: 30 to 60 s of real outdoor rain under a verandah or on a ute canopy, phone-recorded, one unbroken layer from 0 to the end at about -45 dBFS, ducking to -50 under the phone and desk scenes (10.35 to 25.5). Never let the bed drop to digital silence.
- SFX are on the music/SFX stem, not the VO stem: rain on corrugated iron (0 to 3.0, 6.6 to 10.35, 25.5 to end), sleeve crinkle and a single drip 'plip' (4.71), snip (6.85), paper flap and fall (7.0 to 7.77), four cable-tie zips (7.84, 7.93, 8.02, 8.11), soft taps (generic, not iOS) on every tick, a small success chime (14.13 and 17.82), printer slide (23.25), tag creak (27.95).
- EQ for a phone voice memo: high-pass about 90 Hz, -2 to -3 dB high shelf from about 10 kHz, light de-ess. Compression 2:1 to 3:1, 2 to 4 dB of gain reduction. No reverb plugin; if it sounds too clean, worldize it through a small speaker outdoors and blend 20 to 40%.
- Master: VO mono and centred, 48 kHz, reel at about -14 LUFS integrated, true peak -1 dBTP; VO 6 to 8 LU above music.
