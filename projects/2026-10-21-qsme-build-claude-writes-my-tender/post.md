# What your AI assistant can do in QSME, and the four things it never will

**The insight in one sentence:** Nobody in construction is scared of AI doing the typing, everyone is scared of AI *sending*, so the best thing about QSME's assistant connection is the list of things it was never wired to do: it can read the programme, draft the tender and fill the sheet, but it can't move a date, a dollar or a subcontractor.

**The joke (one, done well, on the situation):** at 9:40 pm she asks her AI to push the programme back a week, it says it can't, and we hard-cut to the QSME Gantt sitting exactly where she left it: "It can't. *On purpose.*" It pays off twice: the never list ("Change the schedule or delete a project") and a callback to the same untouched Gantt with the hook's reply pinned to it ("9:40 pm, from the top of this video"). Second, smaller laugh: the cursor reaches "Send Tender Invitations" (the button lights up, the arrow becomes a hand), pulls back, comes back... and just waits ("It drafts. A person presses send.").

**Job:** Week 3 long-form, the prime Wednesday UK/Gulf slot. AI is the most argued-about topic on construction LinkedIn; in programme week this answers "who's allowed to move the dates?". LinkedIn native video from the founder's profile (page reposts Thu 08:30 Sydney) and a YouTube upload where the chapter cards become chapters. Silent-first: every beat reads with the sound off; the Kiwi VO is scripted for later (ElevenLabs upgrade pending), ideally read by the founder.

Nine-forty on a Tuesday. Here's what your AI assistant can do in Queue Ess Mee, and the four things it never will. It gets the typing. Not the dates, the money or the subcontractors.

Three. What it can never do. Queue Ess Mee's own list, word for word. Move money or record a payment. Send or publish a tender, or accept a bid. Issue, sign or cancel a contract. So, back at nine-forty, the programme stays where a person put it.

Four. Who did what. Settings. Connected apps. What it did, newest first. Disconnect, and it loses access immediately.

Five. The small print. Not hidden in a footer. If an assistant tells you it has sent, paid or awarded something, it has not. Every draft is a suggestion for a person to check. Works with Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code, Codex or Cursor. ChatGPT needs a paid plan with developer mode.

[confident] The assistant drafts; you decide. It drafts; you send, award, sign, pay, and move the dates. Thirty minutes, on a working project like yours. Book a thirty-minute demo at Queue Ess Mee dot build. What would you let it type first?
