# VO script: Kiwi performance

**Character:** Mel: everyday Kiwi woman, late 30s, a first-time small developer with three Auckland sections on her shortlist and one planner booked for Monday. Dramatised character voiced by AI; she is never presented as a real customer and never gives a testimonial. General NZ accent (not broad, not Australian), dry and under-played, talking a mate through how she picked. No announcer lift, no exclamation marks, no 'game-changer' energy. She is the straight man; the humour is in the situation (the screen says Strong, then shows a road-widening designation) and in the product being honest about its own limits.

**Model & settings:** `eleven_multilingual_v2` · stability 0.45 · similarity 0.75 · style 0 · speed 1 · speaker boost on

**Voice:** the qsme-nz Mel voice from `tools/voices.json` (currently Jenna `7R9vnLAE0ZSpY0xWp0mv`; shortlist in `projects/2026-10-02-qsme-voice-casting`). Same Mel as "Which Dave?", so QSME NZ has one recurring narrator. Optional QS read: not needed; this one is first person.

**Video:** `outputs/address-to-site-report-16x9.mp4` (145.5 s, 16:9, v2). Every time below is in seconds on this file. The two 9:16 cut-downs have their own timed tables further down; they reuse master takes.

**Captions = VO.** Every burned-in caption carries its VO line; where they differ, the caption only quotes the screen a little more fully ('In amber.', 'fee simple'), so a muted viewer reads what Mel says, jokes included ('Bit boring', 'Red, we've met', 'That's something'). Keep the hedges in the read exactly as written: 'Auckland', 'usually', 'indicative only', 'reads as'.

**Casting line:** "It said strong development potential. Then I scrolled. Fair enough."

**Pronunciation:**
- QSME is written **Q-S-me** (said "Cue Ess Me"). qsme.co.nz is **Q-S-me dot co dot en zed**. Never "kiss me", never four spelled letters.
- **beforeUdig** is said "before you dig" (written `before-you-dig` in the line).
- **indicatively**: "in-DIK-a-tiv-lee", said like quoting the screen.
- **21 m** is "twenty-one metres". **licence** is the NZ noun ("LIE-sence").
- **LINZ** is said "linz" (one word, rhymes with "pins").
- **Maunga** is never voiced (it's on screen only).

**Tone guardrails:** dry, under-played, never selling. She never says "AI", "instant", "any address", "LIM", "valuation" or "verified", and (owner rule 2026-10-03) never "free", "no account", "no sign-up" or anything about price: every CTA is "sign up at Q-S-me dot co dot en zed". The honest-limit lines (vo09, vo14, vo18, vo22, vo24, vo27, vo28, vo32, vo33, vo34, vo35, vo39) are said as plain facts, not apologies. The humour punches at Mel (she skimmed past the amber chip) and at the situation, never at the tool's users. Silence is part of the script: leave 5.5 after "Then I scrolled." mostly to the red row, the b-roll at 11.0–13.3, the lower third at 92.5–94.4, the pan down the one pager at 116.3–119.7 and the end-screen tail 140.5–145.5.
