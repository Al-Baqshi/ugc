- No "free", "no fees", "no listing fee", "bid free" or any pricing generosity (owner 2026-10-03).
- Bids are "compared as totals excl. and incl. GST, each bid opens to its own lines"; never "line by line" (truth sheet §5.2).
- "Tradies can get verified"; never "verified tradies" (§4.6, §5.4). No directory or marketplace numbers (§4.6–4.8).
- Site research: full picture Auckland only; zoning for Wellington City, Christchurch, Hamilton; indicative, not a LIM, title search or planning advice (§1.4, §2.1).
- Granny flat rules kept conditional: "up to 70 m²… where it meets the conditions" (§2.2); never "no consent, full stop".
- Money: QSME keeps the record, never holds the money (§10.5).
- **Managed build:** the software's "QSME manages it" flag is off (§10.5 says do not claim "QSME manages your build" *as a product feature*). The owner's 2026-10-14 brief makes it a **service** QSME offers for granny flats and portable homes, so the brochure sells it as a service delivered by people (QS-style consultant + project manager acting for the owner), run on the platform. Recorded in `ventures/qsme-nz.md` with ❓ for the details.

A flat "we beat any price" guarantee needs substantiation under the Fair Trading Act (Commerce Commission: a trader must have reasonable grounds for a comparative price claim when it is made and be able to show the basis). ❓ owner: keep the softened line, or back the stronger one with a written price-beat policy.
