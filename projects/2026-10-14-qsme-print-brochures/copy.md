_The 2026 rules, in plain words
**Up to 70 m² without a building consent, where the site meets the conditions**
Since 15 January 2026 a new single-storey standalone dwelling up to 70 m² can be built without a building consent if the design is simple and meets the Building Code, licensed building practitioners do or supervise the work, you get a PIM from council, and you notify council before you start and when you finish. Resource consent is a separate test, and on a cross-lease the other owners still have to agree. We check all of it before you commit a dollar.

What "managed by QSME" means
# We act like you would, if you did this every week.
A quantity surveyor is the person on a job who looks after the money, the value and the cost risk. That's the seat we take, on your side of the table.
**The usual way
- One lump-sum quote. You can't see what's inside it.
- The trades and suppliers work for the main contractor. The margin on their work is part of your price.
- Materials are whoever the builder usually uses.
- Progress is a phone call and a photo, when you ask.
- The paperwork lives in several inboxes. Council asks for it later.
**Managed by QSME
- One priced schedule you can read. Every line, every rate.
- Trades and suppliers are tendered and contracted to **you**. We manage them for you. No main contractor's margin on top.
- Materials are tendered too. We go for the best material at the best rate, and show you the comparison.
- A live programme, QR site sign-in, a daily log with photos, and a money page that shows estimate against actual.
- Every document, certificate and claim in one record. The council pack is a download.
**Who's on your job**
Licensed building practitioners for the restricted work, a drainlayer, an electrician, a plumber, and whoever your section needs. All tendered on the same lines, all with a profile and a track record on QSME, all signed into site each day.
**How the money moves**
Work is split into milestones. A milestone is invoiced only with proof of work, and you confirm it before it's paid. Your money goes from your bank to theirs. QSME keeps the record, never the cash.
**What you'll have at the end**
A granny flat or portable home that cost what the schedule said, a complete record of who built it and how, the certificates council needs, and a number you can show a buyer or a bank.
**Straight talk**
- Site research gives the full picture for Auckland sections today; Wellington City, Christchurch and Hamilton get zoning; everywhere else gets boundary, area and council.
- Site research is indicative. It is not a LIM, a title search or planning advice. We order the LIM and title with you before you commit.
- "Without building consent" is conditional. If your site doesn't meet the conditions, we'll tell you, and we'll run the consent instead.
- We'll beat a quote on the same specification. If a quote is cheaper because it leaves things out, we'll show you what's missing rather than match it.

## One record, start to finish
01 · Find & feasibility
**Search the address**
Boundary, area and council for any NZ address. Auckland sections get zone, overlays, services and hazards, a development verdict, what the site would require and a rough feasibility. Then "Create project here".
02 · Estimate
**Price the job in one sheet**
A schedule of quantities with a pocket per trade, your own columns and formulas, Excel in and out. Upload the consent pack and ask the plans a question. AI drafts the unpriced lines, flagged for your review.
03 · Tender
**Cut tenders from the estimate**
Private or public, by trade. Every bidder prices the same lines and never sees your rates or budget. Emailed PDF quotes join the comparison: totals excl. and incl. GST, a Lowest tag, each bid opens to its own lines.

**The winning bid becomes the contract**
One click: milestones, retention and the agreement, signed from a link. The other party needs no account to sign. Work agreed off-platform can be added as a contract too.
05 · Programme
**A programme a lender can read**
Filled from the estimate. One trade slips, everything after it moves, and booked tradies confirm the new dates. Rain days, NZ public holidays and the Christmas shutdown built in. Printed PDF for the bank.
06 · Site
**Know who was there**
QR sign-in poster at the gate, inductions for awarded trades, a daily log with photos, project chat with files and voice notes. Site and office see the same record.
07 · Pay & hand over
**Paid on proof of work**
Milestones are invoiced with proof and confirmed before payment, bank to bank. Estimate against actual on the money page. QS reports: cost plan, progress claim, drawdown certificate, cost to complete. Certificates and the CCC pack in one zip.

How a job runs on QSME
# Same lines for every bidder. Same record for every trade.
You're not pricing against someone's guess. The client priced the job once in a schedule of quantities, and every bidder sees the same quantities and units. Your rate is your rate.
1 · Found
**Profile**
Trades, regions, licences, track record. Get verified with an ID check, usually decided within a business day.
2 · Invited
**Opportunities**
Public tenders by trade and start date, plus private invitations sent straight to you.
3 · Priced
**Your bid**
Same lines as every other bidder. Attach documents, set your dates and terms. Drafts save as you go.
4 · Won
**Contract**
Award creates the agreement, milestones and retention from your bid. Sign from a link.
5 · Paid
**Milestones**
Invoice with proof of work. The client confirms, then pays bank to bank. QSME keeps the record.
**On site**
Scan the QR poster at the gate to sign in. Do the induction once. The daily log and photos go on the same record the client sees, so "who was on site Tuesday" has an answer.
**Your track record**
Ratings only come from jobs finished between QSME members, and they go both ways: you rate the client too. Jobs done, finished cleanly, came back for more. Not reviews anyone can write.
**Everything in your pocket**
The iPhone app shows your work at a glance: opportunities, awarded jobs, dates to confirm, project chat, voice notes and photos from site.
**Suppliers: the quantity is where your product gets chosen**
An estimator pricing a job picks products from the marketplace inside their schedule line. If your 90×45 H1.2 is listed with a rate and a unit, it can be picked for 400 lineal metres on a job that hasn't been tendered yet. That is earlier in the job than any merchant quote. Publish what you want seen, keep the rest private, and update by spreadsheet when prices move.
**Agents: a listing that answers the questions buyers ask**
"Can I put a second dwelling on it?" "What zone is it?" "Is it on a cross-lease?" Your listing links to Site research for the address, so the buyer reads it, not you. Auckland sites get the full picture; Wellington City, Christchurch and Hamilton get zoning.
**Straight talk**
- The directories and the marketplace are growing from a small base. Early profiles get seen first.
- Getting verified is an ID check and a review by a person. It isn't a licence check, and it isn't bought.
- The marketplace is a catalogue: buyers get in touch through your shop page. There's no cart or checkout.
- QSME doesn't hold money. Payments are bank to bank; QSME keeps the proof with the milestone.
