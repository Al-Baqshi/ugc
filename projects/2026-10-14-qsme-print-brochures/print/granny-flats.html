The 2026 rules, in plain words

Up to 70 m² without a building consent, where the site meets the conditions

Since 15 January 2026 a new single-storey standalone dwelling up to 70 m² can be built without a building consent if the design is simple and meets the Building Code, licensed building practitioners do or supervise the work, you get a PIM from council, and you notify council before you start and when you finish. Resource consent is a separate test, and on a cross-lease the other owners still have to agree. We check all of it before you commit a dollar.

What "managed by QSME" means

We act like you would, if you did this every week.

A quantity surveyor is the person on a job who looks after the money, the value and the cost risk. That's the seat we take, on your side of the table.

The usual way
  • One lump-sum quote. You can't see what's inside it.
  • The trades and suppliers work for the main contractor. The margin on their work is part of your price.
  • Materials are whoever the builder usually uses.
  • Progress is a phone call and a photo, when you ask.
  • The paperwork lives in several inboxes. Council asks for it later.
Managed by QSME
  • One priced schedule you can read. Every line, every rate.
  • Trades and suppliers are tendered and contracted to you. We manage them for you. No main contractor's margin on top.
  • Materials are tendered too. We go for the best material at the best rate, and show you the comparison.
  • A live programme, QR site sign-in, a daily log with photos, and a money page that shows estimate against actual.
  • Every document, certificate and claim in one record. The council pack is a download.

Who's on your job

Licensed building practitioners for the restricted work, a drainlayer, an electrician, a plumber, and whoever your section needs. All tendered on the same lines, all with a profile and a track record on QSME, all signed into site each day.

How the money moves

Work is split into milestones. A milestone is invoiced only with proof of work, and you confirm it before it's paid. Your money goes from your bank to theirs. QSME keeps the record, never the cash.

What you'll have at the end

A granny flat or portable home that cost what the schedule said, a complete record of who built it and how, the certificates council needs, and a number you can show a buyer or a bank.

Straight talk

  • Site research gives the full picture for Auckland sections today; Wellington City, Christchurch and Hamilton get zoning; everywhere else gets boundary, area and council.
  • Site research is indicative. It is not a LIM, a title search or planning advice. We order the LIM and title with you before you commit.
  • "Without building consent" is conditional. If your site doesn't meet the conditions, we'll tell you, and we'll run the consent instead.
  • We'll beat a quote on the same specification. If a quote is cheaper because it leaves things out, we'll show you what's missing rather than match it.
