# VO script: Kiwi QS walkthrough (later; the master ships silent)

**Character:** Best: the founder, a professional quantity surveyor, in his own voice, recorded on a decent USB or lapel mic in a quiet office (this is the deep-dive and the standing link for demo follow-ups). Fallback (AI): the same mid-career Kiwi QS voice as 'Six bids. Six formats.', 'Why six bids never compare' and 'Action: chase painter' (late 30s to 40s, general urban New Zealand accent, not broad, not Australian). Calm and dry, a colleague walking a developer through what a lender actually reads. Explaining, never selling. An AI voice never speaks as the founder and is never captioned as him.

**Model & settings:** `eleven_multilingual_v2` · stability 0.45 · similarity 0.75 · style 0 · speed 1 · speaker boost on

**Video:** `outputs/a-programme-a-lender-can-read-16x9.mp4` (128.1 s, 16:9). Seconds on outputs/a-programme-a-lender-can-read-16x9.mp4 (128.1 s). The page is authored on a 132.5 s clock and an edit list (motion/lender.js → SKIPS/HOLDS) maps the file onto it: the title's hold and the middle of every chapter card are cut, chapter 3 holds three still frames for its captions, and two long holds in chapter 5 are cut. These are real file times (window.__toReal(authored) converts).

**Casting line:** “Your programme. As the lender sees it. One contract, counted six times. Q-S-me dot build.”

**Silent cut:** every line is already on screen (big hook and title type, chapter cards, burned-in captions, end card), so the video works with the sound off. This VO is added after the ElevenLabs upgrade, or read by the founder. Owner CTA rule (2026-10-03): no 'free', no pricing or fee lines in any take; the CTA is 'Book a 30-minute demo at qsme.build'.

**Series rule:** The burned-in captions and the big hook/title type are the subtitles, so every voiced line is read word for word ('A4' is read 'A-four', '30-minute' is read 'thirty-minute'). Chapter numbers are spoken with the card title. Sticky notes, chips, toasts and product wording on the screens are not captions and stay unvoiced.

**Hook note:** the cut now opens mid-action (the printout is on the desk at frame 1), so 'Your programme. As the lender sees it.' is one line over the first 2.1 s while the first red ring lands. The calendar's longer 'This is your programme. And this is what the lender sees.' no longer fits before the first caption (1.95 s).

**Changed in the 2026-10-04 review pass:** 'Six fixes. One A-four page.' → 'Six fixes. One printout.'; 'One page. The chart and the task list.' → 'The whole chart fits across the A-four.' + 'The task list follows.' (the PDF writes the task list on its own pages); 'Remember the painter? Grey again.' → 'The painter everyone chased? Never purple.' (works cold, no promise about drags); 'The numbers a lender looks for first.' → 'When, how long, how much. ## Make it sound recorded, not synthesised

- **Founder read (preferred):** one continuous take of the block above, standing, phone or lapel mic 20 cm away, a soft room (curtains, not a meeting room). Read it twice; keep the second. Slice to the start times above with `tools/mux`.
- **AI fallback (after the upgrade):** v2, one request per chapter (hook 01–05, title 06–07, then one request per chapter card + its lines), with `previous_text` set to the line before and `next_text` to the line after, so the chapters keep one voice. Fixed seed per request, three takes, pick by ear. v3 only as a fallback, as one continuous request, sliced afterwards.
- Trim leading and trailing digital silence; never time-stretch more than about 4%. If a take runs past `max_s`, use another take. The chapter reads (vo08, vo23, vo32) are tight because the cards are now 1.2 s: they may run a few tenths into the screen.
- Leave the silences: the import clicks, the drag, the confirm click, the rain push, the tick of 'What drives the finish date', the shutdown band landing, the paper landing on the desk (102.2–102.85), the page flick, the highlighted sentence holding on its own (115.3–117.35) and the pen tracing the rings (117.35–118.8) all play without a voice.
- Pronunciation: QSME = 'Q-S-me' (kyoo-ess-mee), never 'kiss me'; qsme.build = 'Q-S-me dot build'; A4 = 'A-four'; 'Mid-January' with a light hyphen.
