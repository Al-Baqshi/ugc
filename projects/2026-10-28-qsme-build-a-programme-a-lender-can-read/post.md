# A programme a lender can read

**The insight in one sentence:** The one question a lender asks before a drawdown review, "what is actually holding this build up?", usually has no answer on the page we send them (a Gantt screenshot with no dates on the bars, running off the A4, one contract counted six times), so we fix the programme in six places until the page answers it in one printed sentence.

**The joke (light, always on us):** the PM's own screenshot, "Programme_Rev4_FINAL.xlsx", printed for the lender and marked up in red: "dates?", "ends where?", "one 450k contract × 6?". Then the deadpan asides: "The painter everyone chased? Never purple." (a nod to week 3 that works cold) and "Mid-January was never real."

**Job:** Week 4 long-form, the deep-dive payoff to week 3's "Action: chase painter" short (the same way "Why six bids never compare" paid off "Six bids"). It goes out in money week, framed around the funder's drawdown review: prime Wednesday morning UK/Gulf on LinkedIn (founder's profile, the page reposts Thu 08:30 Sydney) and YouTube, where the chapter cards become chapters and the video becomes the standing link for demo follow-ups. Silent-first: every beat reads with the sound off; the Kiwi VO is scripted for later. Since the 2026-10-04 review it no longer depends on week 3 being live: the title kicker is gone and the painter line works cold.

## The programme story (so a QS can check it)

Fictional demo project "Riverside Terraces — Stage 3" (developer: Demo Developments, letterhead marked "Demo letterhead · fictional"), an Australia-org project with no public-holiday calendar, the default Christmas shutdown switched off, and a Monday-to-Friday week. **On site from Mon 6 Jul 2026. "Today" on every in-build screen is Wed 14 Oct 2026, three months in** (the owner shifts the whole demo so "today" is the recording day). 23 SOQ jobs in 7 pockets are imported end to end (finish Wed 28 Apr 2027), then linked by hand (finish Mon 11 Jan 2027). Then, all on "today": Insulation slips 3 working days (finish Thu 14 Jan 2027; Plasterboard and Stopping & finishing are booked with Demo Linings Ltd, so both are sent for their OK; Plasterboard is confirmed on screen, Stopping & finishing is still waiting), yesterday's rain day goes on the cladding (grey, so the finish doesn't move), Kitchens & joinery takes a day longer (purple, so the finish moves a day to Fri 15 Jan 2027), and the site's own "Year-end shutdown" 21 Dec 2026 – 8 Jan 2027 is added as days off (8 jobs move later; the finish moves three weeks to Fri 5 Feb 2027).
