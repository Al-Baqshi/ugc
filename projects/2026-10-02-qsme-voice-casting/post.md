# Kiwi voice casting

Covers **both** QSME NZ ("Which Dave?") and qsme.build ("Six bids. Six formats."). You asked for Kiwi voices that are very natural and don't sound like AI.

**How to choose:** play the `*-preview.mp3` files in the gallery below. These are the voice owners' own samples (not our lines yet), recorded by real New Zealanders and cloned professionally. Pick **one Mel**, **one QS** and optionally **one bloke**. Listen for: real NZ vowels (not Aussie, not British), relaxed rhythm, no "announcer lift" at the ends of sentences.

**Why these:** I can't listen to audio, so this shortlist is built from ElevenLabs data. Every voice here is a professional clone of a real person, with New Zealand English *verified by ElevenLabs*. I favoured conversational/casual voices over narrators, voices proven in heavy use, and long removal-notice periods (so the voice can't vanish from a running campaign).

## What happens after you pick
1. **Upgrade ElevenLabs to a paid plan (Starter is enough).** The free plan blocks library voices through the API ("Free users cannot use library voices via the API") and has no commercial licence, so nothing made on it can go in a QSME ad.
2. I add your picks to the account, then generate every VO line from the timed Kiwi performance scripts (`vo-script.md` in each project). QSME is written as **Q-S-me** so it's said "Cue Ess Me". I check each take with ElevenLabs speech-to-text.
3. I lay the VO onto the two existing videos at the right timestamps, and you listen to the full cut.

## The most natural option of all
With a paid plan you can **clone your own voice** (or a consenting Kiwi tradie/QS on the team) from a few minutes of phone audio. A real person's own voice is the least "AI" thing possible, it's ownable (no library voice can disappear), and it needs no AI-voice disclosure question.
