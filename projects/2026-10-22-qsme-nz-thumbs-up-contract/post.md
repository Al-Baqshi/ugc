# Our whole contract is a thumbs-up

**The joke / insight in one sentence:** Half the small jobs in New Zealand are agreed by text and sealed with a thumbs-up, and a thumbs-up is a very confident answer that doesn't actually say yes.

**The joke:** "Heat-pump wiring in that?" gets 👍 from the sparky. Yes? Or just "seen"? We read the thread back as a legal document, deadpan: "THE REWIRE CONTRACT — Complete and unabridged.", where every clause is a thumbs-up, two of them marked "(unclear)" in red marker. Then QSME writes the thread up properly and refuses to guess: what was said goes in, and what wasn't stays a **[blank]**. The owner types the heat-pump line and fills every blank herself, presses "Send for signing", and Jono signs on his phone. The payoff is a callback: "Everyone has signed. 👍 (clear)", and the end card "Turn the 👍 into a signature." The joke is always the emoji, never the sparky or the owner: Jono answers every message, the emoji just doesn't say enough.

**Job:** Organic first. Week 3's funny short, posted Thursday 6:15 am when sparkies and tradies scroll in the ute before 7, then re-shared to Stories and Facebook at 7:30 pm for owners. It lands just before Labour Day weekend and the pre-Christmas rush of small jobs agreed by text. CTA pushes sign-ups (owner rule 2026-10-03): **Sign up at qsme.co.nz**.

## Music and sound direction

- **Shape:** the hook has no music, only the pop, the zoom whoosh and the gavel tap. Under the contract reading, a dry, deadpan bed: muted plucked bass and a soft brushed snare, about 96 BPM, sparse, "office-comedy" not slapstick. It drops out completely for the signature line (5.12–5.6) so "Agreed. Just never signed." lands in near-silence. It comes back brighter on the whip to QSME (7.06) with a light Rhodes or ukulele motif, and carries the 3.6 s hold on the live quote. Under the finger signature (22.1–23.1) it's just the pen scratch and the tick, then a soft success chime and the bed resolves on the end card. No logo sting.
- **SFX list:** a soft pop, zoom whoosh, gavel tap, paper slide, three rubber-stamp thuds (one per thumb), two marker squeaks, a pen scratch, seven whips (paste, out to the quote, back, up to the phone, to the list, to the end card), keyboard paste and two clicks, highlighter swipes, three soft ticks, typing, quick typing bursts for the blanks, a click on "Send for signing", a tap on the inbox row, scroll flick, pen-on-glass scratch, a checkbox tick, a tap, a success chime, a paper slide for the certificate, a pen stroke on the end card.
- **Source:** one commercially licensed bed used in every version: ElevenLabs Music on a plan that permits commercial use (prompt: *"deadpan office comedy, muted plucked bass and brushed snare, 96 BPM, no vocals, sparse and dry, drops out for one bar, then lifts with a light Rhodes motif, resolves on a soft held chord, 32 seconds"*), or a business-tier Epidemic Sound / Artlist licence. SFX from ElevenLabs SFX (generic: never the iOS keyboard click, the iMessage "sent" sound or any platform's notification sound). Don't add in-app music. Keep prompts and licence receipts in `outputs/licences/`.
- **Mix:** VO about 30 LU above room tone; the bed 12–15 dB under the VO.
