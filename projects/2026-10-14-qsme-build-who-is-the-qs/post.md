Description:
Who's the quantity surveyor on your job? Careful: it might be you.

On a traditional job, the client's QS (often called a PQS) builds the tender, often a bill of quantities; the main contractor's estimator or QS prices it and sends trade packages to subcontractors; the client's QS checks the bids and recommends, and the client decides.

On lots of jobs, especially smaller ones, nobody's hired a QS. So the architect, the engineer, the builder or the developer does the QS job.

QSME is built for both: one estimate (from Excel, a template, or an AI draft you check), tenders where bidders price your lines without seeing your rates, bid comparison, award with payment stages and retention, agreements signed from a link, then the programme, the daily log and the money. Inspired by the QS, but a whole platform: run the job as QS, project manager or client, A to Z.

Book a 30-minute demo: https://qsme.build
