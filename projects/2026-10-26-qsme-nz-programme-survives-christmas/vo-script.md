# VO script: Kiwi performance

**Character:** Mel: everyday Kiwi woman, late 30s, the same dry narrator as the other QSME NZ videos. Here she is talking a mate (a small Auckland developer doing four townhouses) through his programme, in the second person: 'your spreadsheet', 'your buyer'. General NZ accent, never a caricature, no announcer lift. The cold open is deadpan: she treats the spreadsheet as a keen worker and doesn't laugh at her own joke. The product lines sound like the same person explaining something useful across a smoko table. The 'Congratulations' is bone-dry, the only line with a smile in it. Optional A/B: a relaxed Kiwi bloke reads the same words.

**Model & settings:** `eleven_multilingual_v2` · stability 0.45 · similarity 0.75 · style 0 · speed 1 · speaker boost on

**Videos:** `outputs/programme-survives-christmas-16x9.mp4` (the 120.4 s master, `vo-script.json`) and `outputs/january-lie-9x16.mp4` (the 33.0 s 9:16 cut, `vo-script-9x16.json`). Times are seconds on each file.

**Casting line:** “Four townhouses, one spreadsheet, and it reckons we're handing over on the fifteenth. Yeah, nah.”

**Silent cut:** both MP4s ship silent with every line already on screen (headline, burned-in captions, end card 'A finish date you can still say out loud in January.' + 'Sign up at qsme.co.nz'), so they work with the sound off. This VO is added later, after the ElevenLabs upgrade (or recorded by a real Kiwi voice). Owner CTA rule (2026-10-03): no 'free' or pricing line in any take.

**Pronunciation:** QSME = “Q-S-me” (kyoo-ess-mee), never 'kiss me' or four letters. qsme.co.nz = “Q-S-me dot co dot en zed”. 'Bach' = 'batch'. Never say 'critical path', a bank, a lender, SMS/texts, push notifications, 'the app' (the tradie answers on QSME's website or by email), or a count of working days.

**Product order (2026-10-04 review):** in QSME the Christmas shutdown is ON by default, so the script says so, switches it off to match the spreadsheet *before* the template goes on, then switches it back on. Days off only ever move work later, and only when you press the button.

## Make it sound recorded, not synthesised

- Time base: every time is seconds on outputs/programme-survives-christmas-16x9.mp4 (120.4 s, silent master with burned-in captions). The 9:16 cut has its own script: vo-script-9x16.json (outputs/january-lie-9x16.mp4).
- Generation (v2 default): one request per line with request stitching (previous_text = the line before, next_text = the line after; no next_text on vo04, vo14, vo29 and vo31 so they end final). Fixed seed per take, 3 takes per line, pick by ear. Use the continuous read for casting and as the consistency reference. Eleven v3 only as a fallback, as ONE continuous request, sliced afterwards.
- Trim digital silence from each slice; never time-stretch more than ~4%. If a take runs past max_s, use another take. Keep a quiet inhale at the head of lines that follow a silence (vo01, vo05, vo11, vo14, vo16, vo23, vo27, vo30), pulled down 6–10 dB.
- Room tone bed (your own quiet room on the same phone, 30–60 s looped) from 0 to the end at about -48 to -45 dBFS; it fills the gaps (e.g. 12.2–12.7, 44.4–44.9, 102.4–102.9) so silence never drops to digital zero.
- EQ for a phone voice memo: high-pass ~90 Hz, gentle -2 to -3 dB shelf from ~10 kHz, 1–2 dB dip around 6–8 kHz + light de-ess. Compression 2:1–3:1, ~15 ms attack, ~100 ms release, 2–4 dB GR. No reverb.
- Music: none under the cold open (a single sleigh-bell shake at 0.3 that cuts off dead, then office-quiet room tone and marker squeaks). A warm acoustic bed from the title (12.4), steady 6–8 LU under the VO; drop it for the celebration's 0.5 s gap before 'Congratulations' (44.6) and bring it back with a small lift on the confetti; resolve on a held chord over the end card.
- Master: VO mono, centred, 48 kHz; programme ~-14 LUFS integrated, true peak -1 dBTP.
- Time base: seconds on outputs/january-lie-9x16.mp4 (33.0 s). Same voice, settings and processing as vo-script.json; v01–v04, v08 and v10 can be cut from the master's takes (vo01–vo04, vo12, vo14) if the timing fits.
- A single sleigh-bell shake at 0.25 under the hat, then room tone; music from the whip (10.3), confetti lift at 26.2, resolve by 33.0.
