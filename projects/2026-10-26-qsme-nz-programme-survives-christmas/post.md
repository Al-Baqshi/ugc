# How a programme survives Christmas

**The insight in one sentence:** Every NZ programme that crosses Christmas is optimistic on paper because the spreadsheet is the only one on the job working Christmas Day, and the most useful thing QSME does all year is give you a *later* finish date, the real one, before you promise January to a buyer.

**The joke:** A generic spreadsheet is cast as the keenest worker on the job: a marker Santa hat on its 25 Dec cell, red rings on "Labour Day", "Boxing Day" and "New Year's", "Didn't even go to the bach." and "Handover 15 Jan. She'll be right." The callback lands mid-film: "Only 4 are public holidays. Your spreadsheet called the rest working days." Then the twist: QSME moves the finish *out* to Wed 3 Feb, and the film throws it a party ("Congratulations. Your finish date just got later." + confetti, then "It was always February."). The humour stays on the spreadsheet and the calendar, never on the developer, the buyer or the trades.

**Job:** Organic. Goes out on Labour Day itself (Mon 26 Oct, 7 pm), so "Worked Labour Day" lands the day it happens. 2-minute YouTube/Facebook walkthrough for NZ small developers and owner-builders setting programmes that cross Christmas; the 33 s 9:16 "The January lie" carries beats 1–3 to Reels and TikTok. Every CTA pushes sign-ups (owner rule 2026-10-03).

**What changed from the calendar brief (and why):**
- **The shutdown order follows the product (review 2026-10-04).** In QSME the Christmas shutdown is ON by default on every project (truth 7.11), and days off only ever move work later. So the film opens chapter 01 on that default ("QSME shuts the site 23 Dec – 11 Jan by default."), switches it off to match the spreadsheet *before* the template goes on, lands on "Finishes Wed 20 Jan", then switches it back on. That order can be reproduced on screen in Session C (Shots A–C below), and it puts the strongest true line (QSME shuts the site by default) in the film.
- **The Santa-hat cell is "25 Dec · Painting: first coats", not "Framing".** Framing on Christmas Day with a 15 January handover reads as impossible to any builder. The demo job is four townhouses at linings/fit-out stage in October (today's line sits on Labour Day), so a mid-January handover is the kind of optimistic-but-plausible promise the film is about.
- **The amber count is the demo's own: "5 jobs are planned on days the site is shut".** The 12 in the truth sheet is an example. Our five are worked out from the demo programme with the real rules (Mon–Fri, NZ holidays on the day the country takes off, shut 23 Dec–11 Jan inclusive). No caption or VO says the number, so the owner's recording can show whatever the demo returns.
- **The finish date moves Wed 20 Jan → Wed 3 Feb (shutdown) → Thu 4 Feb (Auckland Anniversary added).** All dates follow the rules: Christmas Fri 25 Dec, Boxing Day Mon 28 Dec (Mondayised), New Year's Fri 1 Jan, Day after New Year's Mon 4 Jan (Mondayised), Auckland Anniversary Mon 1 Feb 2027 (not built in), Waitangi Day Mon 8 Feb 2027 (Mondayised). The Labour Day screen is the same project at the same moment (FINISHES Wed 3 Feb, 4 waiting for OK). The 9:16 cut never adds Anniversary Day, so its schedule never shows that band.
- **Two real, public Knowledge Hub captures** (no login) carry the holiday and purple-chain beats: "Days off" on /knowledge-hub/schedule and "The purple chain — what drives the finish date" on /knowledge-hub/schedule-colours.
- **The Public holidays setting is not drawn inside the Days off dialog** (the Knowledge Hub says it lives under Schedule settings → Public holidays). The shutdown band is labelled with the feature's own name, "Christmas shutdown".
- **"Crew away" is left out of the film for time** (it's in the owner's Shot F as optional coverage). The purple beat drags a purple task one day *later* (the documented demo), then presses Undo; the finish-sooner advice ("Shorten a purple one") is shown on QSME's own live guide, in its own words ("Want to finish sooner?").
- **The desk stand-in is gone (review).** The CSS desk read as clip art; the film now ends on the PDF itself: a push-in on the purple key and the "Any one of them running late moves the whole finish." sentence, then a pull back to the whole page for "The date you give the buyer. On one page." If the owner's hands-only desk take (I-desk) comes in, it replaces the last 3 s of that pull-back.
- **B-roll:** title over th-5b-lockup (the four townhouses at lock-up = "the same job"), a th-6-fitout cutaway (a painter, after "Nobody's painting on Christmas Day"), end card over th-7-complete. th-4-structure (framing) no longer fits the late-stage story. All labelled "Illustrative b-roll" in the top-right label slot.
- **CTA:** "Sign up at qsme.co.nz". No "Free to join" anywhere (screen, VO, captions, titles, hashtags, thumbnails).
