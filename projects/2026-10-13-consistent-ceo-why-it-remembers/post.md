Your AI remembers you. But does it know your six businesses?

Most AI is built around chats. Consistent CEO is built around your businesses: each one gets its own story, goals and house rules. Give it a goal and it plans the missions and tasks to get there. Each business keeps short-term memory plus long-term lessons, recalled by meaning from a vector database on your Mac. Same story, same rules, same goals, every chat, every check-in. That's structure. That's why it's consistent.

Jump in. It's open source (MIT) and free for everyone to use. Bring your own AI: your Claude or ChatGPT plan, or a local model.
