# One tender document, fifteen languages: what do you call it?

**The joke / insight in one sentence:** On one international job the same tender document has five names (schedule of quantities, جدول الكميات, 工程量清单, Leistungsverzeichnis, Metraj cetveli), and every one of those people asks the same question in the same email thread, "Prelims included??". So a yellow sticky with that question stays stuck to the screen, untranslated and unmirrored, while qsme.build changes the word, and even the reading direction, around it.

**Truth:** Construction is the same everywhere, but the vocabulary isn't, and software that uses the wrong trade word loses trust before the demo starts.
**Twist:** The site really does use each trade's own word, and mirrors itself for Arabic and Persian. Then it says plainly that the app speaks three languages so far. The Kiwi narrator gives up on "Leistungsverzeichnis" (the self-own), and the sticky note never moves.
**Job:** Organic awareness for non-English-first contractors, QS/PM practices and multinational teams that turns into demo bookings. Success = comments answering "What do you call it where you work?" in the first hour, plus 30-minute demos booked at qsme.build.
