## Make it sound recorded, not synthesised

- Primary source: render continuous_read_v2 three times on the chosen voice, then slice it and place the pieces on the timeline. Use per-line renders only as patches, on eleven_multilingual_v2 with previous_text (the line before) and next_text (the line after) so the intonation carries over. From each set, keep the least polished take. Reject any take where every word gets equal stress or where the product or CTA line lifts into an announcer read. Also reject any edit where two consecutive 'One's…' lines have the same pitch onset and fall.
- Room tone bed: record 60s of a real room on a phone (laptop fan, distant traffic, no music or voices). Run it under the whole video (29.8s on the 4:5, 30.3s on the 9:16) at about -50 to -55 dBFS, silences included, so the dead air sounds live and never drops to digital zero. Crossfade every VO cut 10 to 20 ms into the bed.
- EQ: high-pass at 90 to 100 Hz (12 dB/oct). Cut 200 to 300 Hz by about 2 dB if it's boomy. Add a gentle +1 to 2 dB of presence at 3 to 4 kHz. A small -2 dB high-shelf above 10 kHz takes off the too-clean TTS 'air'.
- Compression: light, 2:1 to 3:1 with 3 to 4 dB of gain reduction, about 15 ms attack and 100 ms release. Bring the VO to about -16 LUFS integrated, -1 dBTP. Don't push the limiter hard enough to pump.
- No reverb and no telephone band-pass (that sounds like a phone call, not a phone recording). If it still sounds 'in the box', play the VO from a small speaker in a furnished room, re-record it on a phone about 40 cm away, and blend that 15 to 25% under the dry track.
- Keep the breaths and tiny mouth sounds. Don't run noise reduction or Voice Isolator on TTS. Only fix obvious artefacts such as clipped word endings, a metallic 's', or a stretched 's' before an ellipsis.
- Placement: start each line 0 to 4 frames before its first caption, and vary the offset by a frame or two so the sync doesn't look machine-quantised. Slice 02, 03, 05, 06, 07 and 09 at their internal pause and place the second half at the time in its delivery note. At natural pace a second half may trail its caption by up to about 0.5s, but it should never lead it by more than 3 frames. Never time-stretch to hit a mark; regenerate or shorten the gap instead.
- Deadpan section (0 to 20.15s): the VO sits alone over room tone, with no music. SFX stay synced to picture and are ducked 10 to 12 dB under any word they overlap: the hook pings (0.95 to 1.86) under 'Six formats', the trackpad click (4.75) under 'spreadsheet', the highlighter squeak (11.8 to 12.2) under 'page nine', and the marker underline squeak (14.25 to 14.68) under 'of a whiteboard' (duck about 12 dB, or drop it). The page flicks (11.1 to 11.65) sit in the gap. Keep the snap-zoom hold (15.0 to 15.85) clean.
- Music: first downbeat at 20.56, on the fourth bidder sheet landing (the sheets land every 0.13s from 20.17; a soft UI tick on each is optional), so the email's silent hold (about 19.4 to 20.56) stays clean. Sit it about 18 dB under 'Same lines for every bidder.' and 'Six bids. One table.', let it rise slightly through the silent click-and-award beat (24.3 to 27.2: row F click 24.75, 'Award bid' 25.35, 'Award → contract created' 25.45), duck it again under the CTA, and resolve on one chord after 'build' (about 29.3).
- Ride levels by ear rather than normalising every line to the same peak. 'Honestly?' and 'there's nothing below' should sit 1 to 2 dB softer than the count lines.
- Bounce a mono VO stem at 48 kHz/24-bit and lay it onto outputs/six-bids-linkedin-4x5.mp4 as a separate track, so music and SFX stay adjustable.
