# VO script: Kiwi performance

**Character:** Mel, the QSME NZ voice (same casting as 'Which Dave?'): everyday Kiwi woman, late 30s, General NZ accent, never a caricature. Here she narrates like a mate who has priced a few builds: calm quantity-surveyor common sense, dry, a little amused at the consent pack and the old spreadsheet, never a presenter. She talks ABOUT the owner-builder ('she', 'her'), never as her: no 'I', no testimonial. Product lines sound like the same person explaining how it works over a bench: no announcer lift, no tagline cadence, no exclamation marks. Optional A/B: a relaxed Kiwi bloke (low, flat, bone-dry) reads the same words.

**Model & settings:** `eleven_multilingual_v2` · stability 0.45 · similarity 0.75 · style 0 · speed 1 · speaker boost on

**Video:** `outputs/pricing-a-build-16x9.mp4` (the complete 135.4 s silent master, re-cut 2026-10-04 after review; times below are seconds on this file). The three 9:16 cut-downs have their own timed lines below (same words, shorter).

**Casting line:** “Consent pack thick enough to prop a door open, and a spreadsheet from twenty twenty-four. Right. Let's price it once.”

**Silent cut:** every master ships silent with every line already on screen as a burned-in caption or headline, so it works with sound off. This VO is added later, after the ElevenLabs upgrade (or recorded by a real Kiwi voice). Owner CTA rule (2026-10-03): no 'free', 'free to join' or any pricing line in any take. Pronunciation: QSME = 'Q-S-me' (kyoo-ess-mee), qsme.co.nz = 'Q-S-me dot co dot en zed', never 'kiss me'.

## Make it sound recorded, not synthesised

- Time base: seconds on outputs/pricing-a-build-16x9.mp4 (135.4 s). Cut-down lines are timed on their own files. Generate with request stitching (previous_text / next_text = neighbouring lines), 3 takes per line, fixed seed logged per take. Trim digital silence, lay over light room tone, never time-stretch more than about 4%; if a take overruns max_s use another take. Mix VO about 12–15 dB above the music bed. If an ElevenLabs voice is used, switch on YouTube's altered/synthetic content disclosure where it applies and Meta's AI label.
- Say 'Christchurch' as Kiwis do (Chryst-church), 'beta' as bee-tuh, 'GST' and 'AI' as letters, 'en zed' not 'zee'.
- Keep the read under-played: this is a mate explaining a spreadsheet at the bench, not a product video.
