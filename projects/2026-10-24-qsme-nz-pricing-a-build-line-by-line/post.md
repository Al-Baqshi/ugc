# Price it once: from the consent pack to one scope every trade prices

**The insight in one sentence:** Everyone prices a build from someone else's spreadsheet and a consent pack nobody has read cover to cover, and the best thing QSME does here isn't a number, it's a gap: "Heads up: no drainage found in this pack", found before the tender, not in a trench.

**The joke:** The consent pack is "thick enough to prop a door open", the budget is her brother-in-law's spreadsheet "from two years ago", and neither one mentions drainage. The humour stays on the paperwork, never on her, the designer, the brother-in-law or the trades. The payoff is a dry callback: the drainlayer gets pointed at the drainage pages, "the ones that exist now".

**Job:** Organic. A 2¼-minute YouTube walkthrough for owner-builders planning summer builds over Labour Day weekend, findable on searches like "how to price a house build NZ". It stops at what the bidder sees. Comparing the bids (20 Oct video) and the agreement (22 Oct video) are linked from cards and the end screen, never named by day, so this one stays evergreen. Three 9:16 chapter cuts carry it to Shorts. Every CTA pushes sign-ups (owner rule 2026-10-03).

**What changed from the calendar brief (and why):**
- The CTA is **"Sign up at qsme.co.nz"**, not "Free to join". The owner CTA rule of 2026-10-03 overrides the slot text, so no "free" appears anywhere (screen, VO, captions, titles, hashtags).
- **No Chat with AI beat.** We can't confirm the real answer carries a "From your documents (… p.N)" label, so per the brief the beat is cut (review 2026-10-04). It comes back only if Shot G shows a real answer with a source label. Never mock a citation.
- **No homepage captures.** The two public-homepage stills were mostly empty dark page with sliced copy, so they're dropped; the chapter pill and a short lead-in on the app carry each step change.
- **Drainage arrives before pricing.** The designer's drainage sheets and their read-in now sit straight after "she asks the designer", so Step 2 prices drainage lines the sheets actually support.
- The pointer to the comparing-bids video is **evergreen**: "Comparing the totals on the same GST basis? That video's linked here ↗" + a YouTube info card. No "Tuesday".
- The bidder shot shows **"Itemize" chosen**, and the small tag says "They can itemise or quote one total." Bids are never shown compared line by line.
- The **® is dropped** from the end-card wordmark (registration unconfirmed, same as the other NZ videos).
