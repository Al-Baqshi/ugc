# VO script: Kiwi performance

**Character:** Mel: everyday Kiwi woman, late 30s, renovating her first place. Organised, a bit worn down by the thread but amused by it. She's the straight man, talking to a mate about her week. General NZ accent, never a caricature. She stays dry and under-played. The roll call is flat and bored, with a natural downward drift, and the product lines sound like the same person: no announcer lift, no tagline cadence. Optional A/B: a relaxed everyday Kiwi bloke reads the same words as a narrator. His hook is 'She asked the community page for a builder.'

**Model & settings:** `eleven_multilingual_v2` · stability 0.45 · similarity 0.75 · style 0 · speed 1 · speaker boost on

**Video:** `outputs/which-dave-30s-9x16.mp4` (the complete 30 s reel, rebuilt 2026-10-02; times below are seconds on this file, hook included). `outputs/which-dave-30s-4x5.mp4` runs on the same clock, so one stem fits both. The old `outputs/which-dave-reel-body-9x16.mp4` is superseded.

**Casting line:** “Bathroom and a little deck. Six Daves later, still no builder. Bit much, eh.”

**Silent cut:** the MP4 ships silent with every line already on screen (the hook kicker, the comments, the captions, the end card), so it works with sound off. This VO is added later, after the ElevenLabs upgrade.

## Make it sound recorded, not synthesised

- Time base: every time in this script is in seconds on outputs/which-dave-30s-9x16.mp4, the complete 30 s reel (hook included, no +2.5 offset any more). outputs/which-dave-30s-4x5.mp4 runs on the same clock, so one VO/SFX/music stem lays onto both with tools/mux.
- Generation (v2 is the default). (a) Hook: one request, no previous_text, no next_text. (b) Roll call: one request, 'Deck Dave. Roof Dave.\nBig Dave. Little Dave.', with previous_text set to the hook line and no next_text. Cut vo01 and vo02 from the same take, and never butt a pair from one take against a pair from another. (c) vo03 to vo06: one request per line, using request stitching. Set previous_text to the line before; for vo03 that is the on-screen beat '...can anyone actually come and quote? Dave can.'. Set next_text to the line after for vo03 and vo04; leave it empty for vo05 (the ding follows, not speech) and vo06, so both end final. (d) Use a fixed seed for each take and log it, so a picked take can be regenerated exactly if a neighbouring line changes. Make 3 takes per request and pick by ear. (e) The continuous read is for casting and as the consistency reference. Use Eleven v3 only as a fallback, as ONE continuous request (casting line as warm-up, then the continuous read with '[exhales]' before 'Or just'), sliced afterwards.
- Trim the leading and trailing digital silence from each slice. Inside each roll-call pair, close the full-stop gap to about 0.2 s with a cut over room tone (a cut, not a stretch). Never time-stretch more than about 4%. If a take runs past max_s, use another take rather than speeding it up.
- Breaths: keep a quiet natural inhale at the head of any line that follows a silence (before Deck Dave, Or and It's free), pulled down 6 to 10 dB. Cut clicks, mouth noise and any breath in the middle of a phrase.
- Room tone bed: record 30 to 60 s of your own quiet lounge or kitchen on the same phone. Run it as one unbroken layer from 0 to the end at about -48 to -45 dBFS. Keep it running through the Dave silence (9.0 to 11.0) and the 'Dave can.' silence (11.9 to 13.3). Silence that drops to digital zero between lines is the biggest AI tell.
- EQ for a phone voice memo: high-pass at about 90 Hz (12 dB/oct), a gentle high shelf of -2 to -3 dB from about 10 kHz, and a small 1 to 2 dB dip around 6 to 8 kHz with a light de-esser. No presence boost.
- Compression: 2:1 to 3:1, attack about 15 ms, release about 100 ms, 2 to 4 dB of gain reduction.
- No reverb or 'room' plugin. If it still sounds too clean, worldize it: play the VO through a small speaker in the room and re-record it on the phone about 50 cm away; blend 20 to 40% under the dry track.
- Hook (0 to 2.5): pings stacking under the count jumps (0.5 and 1.05) and the slam of 'Still no builder.' at 1.3, mixed under the VO. During the roll call (5.7 to 8.85) the VO sits under no music, only the comment pings at 5.55 and 6.9, plus any fling SFX from 8.4 under the trailing 'Little Dave'.
- Music comes in at 13.5 (the end of the swipe). Hold it steady, about 6 to 8 LU under the VO, from 13.5 to 22.2. Dip it for the ding on the tally tick at about 22.95 so the ding plays clean, then bring it back for 'Know which Dave.' (23.75) and the end card (25.45), and resolve by 30.0.
- Place lines by eye and ear, not on a grid. Start each roll-call slice 4 to 6 frames after its comment starts popping, once the ping has decayed (5.7 and 7.1). Treat the other start_s values as targets.
- Master: VO mono and centred, 48 kHz. The reel should land at about -14 LUFS integrated with true peak at -1 dBTP. Dialogue around -14 to -16 LUFS short-term, about 30 LU above the room tone bed and 6 to 8 LU above the music.

(2) 'Or.' as a one-word sentence plus a v3 [sighs] will sound read and won't fit. Use 'Or just post the actual job.' at 10.75, with a breath under the swipe.

(3) The product lines butt together at 0.05 s gaps, each frame-locked to its caption, and the music ducks and swells per line. Together that is ad-read cadence. Space the lines (13.25 and 15.7), hold the music bed steady, and glue the fragments: 'Licence-verified tradies quote on it.', 'Line by line, so it's apples with apples.', 'Put the job up on Q-S-me. (4) For anti-AI generation: speed 1.0 instead of 0.95, v2 request stitching (previous_text and next_text), v3 only as one continuous take with a single [exhales], keep the natural inhales, and fix the room-tone level (VO about 30 LU above it, not 8 to 10).
