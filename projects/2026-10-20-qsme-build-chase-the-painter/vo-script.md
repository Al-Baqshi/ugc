# VO script: Kiwi performance

**Character:** The QS, the same narrator as "Six bids. Six formats." He's a mid-career quantity surveyor (late 30s to 40s) with a general, urban, professional New Zealand accent (Auckland or Wellington office). Not broad, not rural, not Australian. This time he chaired the meeting. He wrote "Chase painter" in the minutes six weeks running, and he's quietly owning it. He's dry and deadpan, amused at himself rather than annoyed at anyone, and firmly on the painter's side. He's a fictional narrator: he never claims to be a QSME customer and never speaks as the founder.

**Model & settings:** `eleven_multilingual_v2` · stability 0.45 · similarity 0.75 · style 0 · speed 1 · speaker boost on

**Video:** `outputs/chase-the-painter-4x5.mp4` (33.8 s, LinkedIn, retimed 2026-10-04 after review). `outputs/chase-the-painter-9x16.mp4` (YouTube Shorts) runs on the same clock, so one stem fits both. Times below are seconds on those files.

**Casting line:** "Six weeks we've chased the painter. Turns out it was the switchboard. Yeah, nah. We owe him a coffee."

**Silent cut:** both MP4s ship silent, with every line already on screen (hook, captions, tags, toasts, end card), so they work with the sound off. This VO gets added after the ElevenLabs upgrade.

## Real-person alternative

This is better for trust and needs no AI label. The founder, or a real QS who agrees to it, reads the 13 lines as a phone voice memo in a quiet room, using the same timings. If it's the founder, "we" is the founder's own team. That's fine, as long as it never implies a real client job.
