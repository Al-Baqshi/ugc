Every build is a chain — one job finishes, the next begins. The Schedule turns that chain into a plan.

Start either way. Price your SOQ, tender the work — tenders and contracts all land here on their own. Or start from the Schedule: lay the plan out, save it as your own template, and run every project like a pro.

Drop a job right into the middle of things — on a day, after another job, or alongside one.

Open any job and see its order of work — what must finish first, what's waiting on it. Even leave a gap: four working days for the slab to cure.

Rained out? Log a rain day — the job moves, and everything after it moves forward with it. The tradies affected are asked first — nothing changes until they say yes.

And it can never tangle. Ask a job to wait for something that's waiting for it — Q S me says no. No loops. No knots.
