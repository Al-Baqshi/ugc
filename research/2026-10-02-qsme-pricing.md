# QSME NZ pricing facts (2026-10-02)

Source: https://www.qsme.co.nz/pricing (read in browser). Use these to keep ad claims accurate.

- "Start free." Free is a plan, not a trial. No revenue share, no per-seat surprises, no trial clock.
- **Owners / developers / consultants:** first project free; **post tenders and take bids with no listing fee**; AI trial uses; inviting your own tradies is always free.
- **Tradies / subbies / suppliers:** bid free forever; unlimited bids on public tenders; nothing to pay if the job never goes ahead.
- **Success fee:** only when QSME's *public* marketplace wins the work. 1% each side, capped at $2,000, invoiced only when the job completes. No fee for your own crew.
- Paid plans are quoted per business (by number of projects): Estimate, Tender, Schedule, Documents & compliance, Chat, Contracts & payment.
