# QSME site review (2026-09-28)

Sources: https://qsme.co.nz and https://qsme.build homepages (read in browser).

## qsme.co.nz: NZ owners/developers + tradies
- Positioning: "Watch your project come to life", from feasibility to CCC. Free to join.
- Features: AI feasibility & estimates, tenders, line-by-line quote comparison, verified trades, progress timeline, documents, payment approvals, AI assistant, "Your project can talk" (plans that answer questions and cite the page), Claude/ChatGPT via MCP, live projects map.
- Two CTAs: "Create a project" (owners) / "Join as a tradie".
- Ready-made lines to reuse: "apples to apples", "not found in a Facebook group", "nothing lives in someone's inbox", "Your project can talk", "It just does the typing".

## qsme.build: global B2B
- Positioning: tendering & estimating software for construction orgs. Estimate = tender = contract = build record.
- Flow: Scope (SOQ) → private tender → line-by-line award → contract + e-sign → Gantt, daily log, QR sign-in. iOS app.
- Audiences: main contractors, QS/PM practices, clients/developers, enterprise/white-label.
- CTA: Book a 30-minute demo.

## Gaps for ads
- No visible social proof on either homepage (testimonials, counts, logos). Testimonial-style ads have to wait for real proof.
- Two brands, two tones: NZ = Kiwi relatable, B2C-ish; .build = industry insider, LinkedIn.
