On 2026-11-15 the owner heard the LB01 takes from Fadi, Mimi and Nizar, all labelled "Lebanese" in the ElevenLabs library, and said: "This is not a Lebanese accent. This is Syrian." They were angry.

**Why:** the owner is close to Lebanese speech and hears the Syrian/Lebanese difference at once. A wrong dialect makes Lebalist content feel fake to its audience.

**How to apply:**
- Never call a voice Lebanese (or any dialect) because of its library label or accent tag. Say it's a candidate until the owner has heard it.
- Send short samples (two or three lines) before full takes, so a bad accent costs seconds of their time, not a full listen.
- Test eleven_multilingual_v2 alongside eleven_v3, because v3 can flatten a voice toward a generic Levantine read.
- A real Lebanese speaker (the owner or a friend, recorded or cloned with consent) is the reliable route.
