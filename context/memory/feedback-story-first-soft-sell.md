On 2026-11-14 the owner rejected the first Lebalist script («بعرفلك واحد» v1) as "too straightforward… too naive", because it had a narrator saying "I opened Lebalist, picked my area…". They want a creative story people enjoy watching for itself, where the viewer works out that the product is the answer and the message stays hidden.

**Why:** the owner thinks a direct pitch inside a UGC skit kills both the enjoyment and the credibility.

**How to apply:**
- For Lebalist, don't explain the product in the voice-over. Use a twist or punchline that implies it, at most one visual clue (a link preview, an app glimpse), and the logo and site on the end card. No spoken CTA unless asked.
- Before writing scripts for other ventures, ask whether they want this too. QSME's audio-first standard still requires a spoken CTA, so this rule doesn't apply there automatically.
