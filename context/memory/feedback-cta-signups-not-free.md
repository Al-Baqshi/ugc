Never write "free", "free to join", "no sign-up", "no login", "without an account", "no listing fee", "your own crew is always free", "bid free", "no fees", "no per-seat fees" or success-fee details in QSME videos, captions, scripts or ads. Every CTA pushes sign-ups (NZ: "Sign up at qsme.co.nz"; Global: "Book a 30-minute demo at qsme.build"). Present site search / site research as what you get when you sign up.

**Why:** the owner (2026-10-03) said the address check / site search requires sign-up and that being "very generous" with free/no-sign-up messaging undermines the goal: awareness that converts to sign-ups.
**How to apply:** check every script/caption/end card for these words before rendering; the venture files carry the same rule. Related: [[project-qsme-content-programme]], [[user-ugc-goals]]
