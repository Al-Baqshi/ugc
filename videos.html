**Pinned reply for "does QSME send it to council?"**
> No. QSME keeps the certificates together and builds the pack: every certificate plus an index, as one zip. You download it and lodge your CCC application with council yourself. QSME doesn't issue certificates or the CCC.

**Pinned reply for "does the tradie need to be on QSME?"**
> Yes. Your tradie adds their LBP licence on their QSME profile; once QSME has approved it, they can record certificates on your project, with the official document they issued through their own certification system attached. Sign up at qsme.co.nz to set up your project.
