**Proposed workflow**
1. Make Lebalist a venture: create `ventures/lebalist.md` from the template. Product facts come from the repo docs; the owner gives audience, voice and humour limits.
2. Ideas first: pitch 3 ideas with hooks in Lebanese Arabic and get a yes before scripting.
3. Script: spoken Lebanese dialect (not MSA) in Arabic script, with natural French/English mixed in. Continuous speech, hook in the first 2 s, and a native check before any voice work.
4. Voice: preferably a real Lebanese voice recorded by the owner or a friend, then forced alignment for timings. Fallback: an ElevenLabs eleven_v3 test take, since it may not do a Lebanese accent.
5. Picture: real app screens from the simulator with the app set to Arabic/RTL, plus a motion page with an Arabic font and Arabic captions taken from the narration. 9:16 first.
6. Usual project folder, vo-script, QA, Studio. Proposed new ID prefix: `L01…`.
